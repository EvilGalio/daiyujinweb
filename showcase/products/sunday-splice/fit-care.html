Know the piece before you choose

Fit starts with
something familiar.

Take a garment you like wearing, lay it flat and compare the actual dimensions. You do not need to enter body measurements or personal information.

01 / PREPARE

Lay it flat.

Fasten a similar garment, smooth it gently and avoid stretching the cloth or waistband. Use a flat surface and measure in centimetres.

02 / COMPARE

Choose like for like.

Compare an overshirt to an overshirt, a tee to a tee and a trouser to a trouser. Layers have different amounts of room built into them.

03 / LOOK AGAIN

Read the other dimensions.

One width is a starting point. Check length, sleeve or inseam too. The tool compares numbers; it does not promise comfort or body fit.

Care between wears

Keep it simple.
Keep the label.

Our intended care specification is a gentle 30°C wash with similar colours, no bleach and no tumble drying. Reshape the garment and let it air dry. Fasten Studio and Loop before washing; avoid stretching the Field neckline.

The physical garment label would always take precedence. Before using an unfamiliar cleaner, stain treatment or iron setting, check the specific cloth and care instructions.

General cool-wash and colour-separation principles were checked against Kowtow's woven-garment care guidance. These original concept specifications are not a test result or an endorsement.

When a detail needs attention

Notice it early.

Loose buttons and open seams are easier to discuss when you can identify the garment and the affected detail. Do not assume every fabric issue has the same repair.

Ordinary buttons and accessible stitching are part of the design. No free repair service, parts stock or lifetime promise is offered by this demonstration.
