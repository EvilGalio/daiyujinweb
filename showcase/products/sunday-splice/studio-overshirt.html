The piece, up close

One layer.
A few useful details.

A pointed collar, two open patch pockets and a straight hem keep the shape simple. Button the front for a shirt-like outline, or wear it open over Field. The cloth has a little more body than our trouser, with a visible twill texture.

How it is put together

Two square chest pockets without flaps; button front and cuffs; straight hem. Unlined, with accessible stitching and ordinary buttons for a future repair discussion.

An everyday layer, not rainwear, protective workwear or a certified performance garment.
