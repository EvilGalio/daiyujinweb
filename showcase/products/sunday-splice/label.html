Close to the making

A small label.
A deliberate range.

We began in Lisbon in 2021, bringing design, sampling and the coordination of small local production runs into one close conversation. The idea was an everyday wardrobe with enough character to feel personal.

Our first three forms have stayed straightforward: an overshirt, a trouser and a tee. We work with proportion, visible texture and colours that can share a rail. More choices are not automatically better choices.

The way we choose details

We think about the pocket you actually use, a neckline that sits simply and a button you can identify later. Accessible stitching and ordinary closures make a future repair conversation easier to begin.

The range is intended for direct retail within the EU, with a relaxed straight cut and clear garment measurements. We do not offer tailoring, rental, performance wear or unlimited made-to-order options.

Before you choose

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

How should I choose a size?

Compare a similar garment laid flat with the listed measurements, then check all the dimensions. The letters XS–L are labels for these particular pieces. They do not replace the measurements or guarantee body fit.

Can I order a different colour or length?

The complete range is the three listed garments, each in two colours and four sizes. Additional colours, alterations and made-to-measure work are outside this collection.

Can I place a real order here?

This is a fictional label and a local shopping demonstration. The bag calculates illustrative item totals and creates a downloadable summary. It has no checkout, payment, stock, shipping or actual order.

Can I contact the label?

The example contact is hello@sundaysplice.example. It is a demonstration address, and this website sends no email or enquiry. Please do not enter personal details in the local tools.
