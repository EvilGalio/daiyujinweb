RESPONSIBILITIES
Review stock exceptions: Compare item records and identify differences that need clarification.
Keep supplier timing visible: Record updated dates and make changes easy for the team to follow.
Prepare replenishment discussions: Bring the available data and its limitations to a conversation about the next order.
Check the item record: Reconcile key fields and document corrections with the appropriate owner.

RELEVANT EVIDENCE
Reconciling item records: An example of finding and investigating a mismatch without assuming which source was correct.
Working with spreadsheets: How you organised a comparison and checked the figures before sharing it.
Communicating across teams: How you explained a timing change or data gap to another team.
