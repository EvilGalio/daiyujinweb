JUNIPER PEOPLE / A USEFUL ROLE CONVERSATION
Fictional company. No vacancy, application or candidate assessment.

READ THE CONTEXT
Check responsibilities, reporting line, team, location, work pattern and what the stated pay includes. In a real process, benefits, the employing entity and full terms would need separate disclosure.

PREPARE A RELEVANT EXAMPLE
Choose a responsibility and a past situation that shows your approach. Explain the context, choices and what you learned. Keep employer information confidential. No documents are uploaded or assessed here.

BRING YOUR QUESTIONS
Who sets priorities? How does the team hand work over? What support would the first week include? What is the purpose of the next conversation and who communicates the decision?

BOUNDARY
All three example roles, employers and pay ranges are invented. Pay is not market advice or an offer. No CV, personal details, salary history, message or application is collected.
