JUNIPER PEOPLE / EMPLOYER PREPARATION CHECKLIST
Fictional company / local demonstration

1. Choose the work: permanent search, hiring review or onboarding workshop.
2. Set out the role responsibilities, location, working pattern and reporting line.
3. Identify relevant evidence and which experience is simply helpful.
4. Name the decision owner and the people involved.
5. Note the number of people, intended timing and remaining scope questions.
6. Discuss fees, timing and any actual engagement separately.

Draft responsibilities, absent decision ownership, an urgent date, more than five people, another region or another function remain specific discussion points. No request automatically fits the scope.

No real search, workshop, hire, application, CV collection, candidate ranking or message is available. Keep company and personal information with you.
