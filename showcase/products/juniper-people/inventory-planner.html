Inventory Planner

Keep item records, supplier timing and replenishment discussions connected. This example role focuses on identifying exceptions and bringing the right context to a planning decision.

JP-103Fictional role example.
Not a vacancy or an invitation to apply.

The work you'll find here.

Review stock exceptions

Compare item records and identify differences that need clarification.

Keep supplier timing visible

Record updated dates and make changes easy for the team to follow.

Prepare replenishment discussions

Bring the available data and its limitations to a conversation about the next order.

Check the item record

Reconcile key fields and document corrections with the appropriate owner.

The evidence we'd discuss.

Use a relevant past-work example to explain how you approached the task. Keep employer information confidential; there is no upload or assessment here.

Reconciling item records

An example of finding and investigating a mismatch without assuming which source was correct.

Working with spreadsheets

How you organised a comparison and checked the figures before sharing it.

Communicating across teams

How you explained a timing change or data gap to another team.

Experience with an ERP system would help. A degree is not required for this example role.

What a conversation
could look like.

  1. Understand the role and context.Talk through the work pattern, reporting line, responsibilities and the questions you would bring.
  2. Explore a relevant example.Discuss the decisions you made in past work and what you learned, without sharing confidential material.
  3. Ask about the team and next steps.Clarify expectations, unanswered questions and who would communicate a decision.

This is an illustrative conversation structure, not a live selection process. Benefits, the actual employing entity and full employment terms would need to be disclosed in any real process.
