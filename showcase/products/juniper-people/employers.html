A growing team does not always need another search. Sometimes it needs a better brief, a clearer decision or a more thoughtful first week. Start with the work in front of you.

For Central Texas companies, generally 25–250 people, hiring permanent operations, support and inventory colleagues. We work with employer-direct roles. Fees, timing and any engagement would be agreed separately.

01 / A new person for your team

Permanent search

Turn a business need into a role people can understand, then agree a focused search for permanent, direct employment.

A search, fees and timing would need separate agreement. No candidate, introduction, hire or start date is promised.

Discuss Permanent searchService guide ↗

Bring the context.

  • The responsibilities that need an owner.
  • The work pattern, location and reporting line.
  • Evidence that matters in the role and who will make the decision.

Know the handover.

  • A clear role and search brief for agreement.
  • A structured conversation agenda tied to actual responsibilities.
  • A proposed sequence of introductions, questions and decisions.

02 / Clarity before another interview

Hiring review

Look at the role description, interview ownership and decision stages together. Find the unclear parts before they become another round of conversations.

This is a practical hiring-process review, not a legal or compliance audit. It does not assess or rank candidates.

Discuss Hiring reviewService guide ↗

Bring the context.

  • The current role description, with open questions marked.
  • The people involved in interviews and decisions.
  • The stages of the process and where ownership becomes unclear.

Know the handover.

  • A prioritised role-clarity and process-change checklist.
  • A map of interview responsibilities and decision handovers.
  • Questions to resolve before a revised process is agreed.

03 / A considered first week

Onboarding workshop

Bring managers together around the work a new colleague will join, the first-week handover and the people who will help them settle in.

This demonstrates a manager planning session. It does not onboard real people, provision accounts or operate an HR system.

Discuss Onboarding workshopService guide ↗

Bring the context.

  • The responsibilities the new colleague will take on.
  • The manager, day-to-day support and handover owners.
  • The first-week context and access questions to resolve internally.

Know the handover.

  • A manager workshop agenda.
  • A first-week responsibilities and support checklist.
  • A list of open handovers and named internal owners to agree.

Where we fit.

Austin, Round Rock and Georgetown. Permanent operations coordination, customer support and inventory planning in regional growing teams.

If the function or region is different, the first step is to discuss fit. We do not turn an out-of-scope request into a promised engagement.

Keep the scope honest.

No temporary staffing, executive search, regulated professional recruitment, payroll, immigration advice or guaranteed placements. Hiring reviews do not provide legal or compliance conclusions.

Any real fee, service agreement, search and schedule would be separately discussed. This concept website provides no live recruitment service.
