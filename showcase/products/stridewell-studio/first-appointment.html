Your first visit

A place to start,
without having
all the answers.

Your first conversation makes time to listen, understand what you want to discuss and explain the next step. You can arrive with questions.

A 50-minute first conversation

From hello
to a clearer plan for the visit.

Our initial format is €78 as an illustrative private session. No treatment or series of future visits is decided by this website.

  1. 01

    Settle in
    10 minutes

    Meet the session team member, get comfortable and begin with the questions you have brought. Explain what you would like to cover rather than feeling you have to use specialist language.

  2. 02

    Talk and look together
    25 minutes

    Discuss the everyday activities that matter to you. Agree what is being considered before any movement is observed. Ask for an explanation, pause or clarification whenever you want one.

  3. 03

    Make room for questions
    15 minutes

    Recap the discussion and what a next step might involve. A supported session or review is not an automatic commitment; the first conversation can simply establish what needs discussing further.

Before the door opens

Bring the practical things.
Leave room for a question.

  • Choose comfortable clothes that let you move easily.
  • Bring the questions you want to discuss in person.
  • Think about arrival, your preferred conversation language and whether you want to bring a companion.
  • Ask about the room and any practical access details before attending.

The general suggestions to wear comfortable clothing and bring questions are consistent with Guy’s and St Thomas’ appointment guidance. Our visit format and studio details are defined separately.
