Common questions

The things you
may want to know.

Who are the sessions for?

The studio scope is local adults aged 18 and above. We explain the three formats, but this website does not assess clinical suitability. Any real suitability decision requires an appropriate individual professional assessment.

Can my first visit be a supported session or a review?

Those formats follow an initial studio visit and an agreed plan. If you choose one for a first visit, the preparation planner keeps your request and marks the first-conversation step to discuss. It does not clinically recommend or approve a session.

Is there an evening or weekend option?

The studio hours are Monday-Thursday 08:30-18:00 and Friday 08:30-16:00, Utrecht local time. Evenings and weekends are outside the stated scope. Selecting that preference creates an opening-hours gap, without reserving a time.

Can I bring a companion or ask for quieter waiting?

Yes, include the practical question in your preparation note so the arrangement can be discussed. No preference automatically reserves extra space or a particular arrival time.

Which languages can I ask for?

The concept studio offers conversations in English or Dutch. Your selection appears in the preparation checklist and downloadable note. No interpreter service is represented.

Are the fees covered by insurance?

No insurance arrangement or reimbursement is represented on this site. The three fees are illustrative private-session amounts. The planner creates no invoice, claim, payment or binding price offer.

Can I send a report, medical history or exercise plan?

Please do not. This website has no health intake, file upload or clinical messaging. The planner has only predefined practical preferences and stores them on the current page until you refresh or leave.

Do you provide home visits, online care or urgent help?

No. The stated service is an appointment-led visit at the studio. There are no home or remote sessions, emergency services, diagnoses or treatment instructions through this website. For urgent medical concerns, use the appropriate local healthcare service; do not wait for this fictional studio.

Does preparing a note book a session?

No. The result is a local checklist you can edit, mark as ready and download. It makes no contact with a real studio. A downloaded file stays on your device; page selections and checklist state clear when you refresh or leave.
