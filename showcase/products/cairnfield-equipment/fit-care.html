Find your fit.

Lay out your usual load, including layers and food. Use the capacity figures as a starting point, then compare the outer dimensions and how your things are shaped. A full bag should close without forcing the zip or compressing contents against you.

Ridge 22

The sample back-length range is 38–52 cm. Adjust the shoulder straps over your usual layer and position the removable sternum strap where it feels comfortable. Actual comfort depends on your body and load; the range is not a fitting prescription.

Moor 8

The sling strap spans 85–140 cm. Compare that distance around your shoulder and usual clothing. Keep the load light enough to carry comfortably on one shoulder; it does not offer the support of the Ridge’s two shoulder straps.

A bag is not a dry bag.

These fabric bags have no sealed seams or certified waterproof rating. Zips and seams can admit water. Keep weather-sensitive contents separately protected, and check conditions before heading out. This collection is not offered for extreme weather or overnight expeditions.

Clean, open, dry.

  1. Empty all pockets and remove loose dirt with a soft brush.
  2. Wipe fabric with cool water and a soft cloth. Use only a little mild soap where needed, then wipe away the residue.
  3. Open the bag fully and air dry away from direct heat before storage. Do not machine wash or tumble dry.

Wipe the foam pad separately. Avoid sharp objects and direct heat; check its retaining band before use.

Notice the small changes.

A stiff zip, a fraying strap or a loose stitch is a reason to stop and inspect the piece. Do not force a damaged closure or keep using a compromised strap.

A real repair conversation would start with the product, location of the damage and how it happened. This concept does not process warranty claims or accept uploaded photographs. The checklist below is a local preparation note.
