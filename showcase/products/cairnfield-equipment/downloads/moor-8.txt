An 8-litre starting point
For a small load on short outings. If your packed things need more than eight litres, choose the Ridge instead of squeezing the zip closed.

A place for the small things
A front zip pocket and internal key loop separate keys from the main compartment. The wide opening makes the remaining space easy to see.

Across the body
The single strap adjusts from 85 to 140 cm. Compare that span over your usual layers; this is a sling, not a two-strap load-carrying pack.

Care
Empty every pocket, brush off loose dirt and wipe with cool water. Let the lining dry fully before storage. Avoid hot washing and a tumble dryer.
