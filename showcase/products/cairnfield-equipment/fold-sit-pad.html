A proper little pause

A 40 × 30 cm open surface for a lunch stop. Choose a level, appropriate place to sit; the pad is not a sleeping mat or protective equipment.

Fold and carry

The 85 g pad folds to 30 × 10 × 4 cm. Put it inside your bag or secure it yourself; no external mounting system is included.

Nothing complicated

Charcoal foam with an orange retaining band. No valve, inflation or electronic parts to look after.

Keep it ready

Wipe with water and a mild soap if needed, rinse and air dry. Keep away from sharp objects and direct heat. Replace a damaged retaining band before relying on it to hold the folded pad.
