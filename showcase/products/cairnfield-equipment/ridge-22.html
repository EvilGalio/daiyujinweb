The everyday load

22 litres for a day of walking. Compare the volume with your own packed kit; a litre figure does not describe every object’s shape.

Things in reach

A front zip pocket separates small items. Two side pockets hold your own bottles; keep heavier contents close to your back.

A simple fit

Padded shoulder straps and a removable sternum strap. The sample back-length range of 38–52 cm is a starting comparison, not a fit guarantee.

Keep it ready

Brush off dry dirt, then wipe with a soft cloth and cool water. Leave open to dry away from direct heat. Do not machine wash or tumble dry.
