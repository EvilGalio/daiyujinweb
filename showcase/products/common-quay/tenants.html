    Before a conversation

    A little context goes a long way.

    Common-area upkeep

    Identify the shared area, describe what needs attention and say whether access is affected. Establish whether the item belongs to the common parts or sits inside the occupier's own unit.

    Access & deliveries

    Prepare the relevant entrance, the broad delivery window and any unusual vehicle or handling needs. Never share keys, entry codes or personal access credentials in an open enquiry.

    Proposed alterations

    Describe the intended change, affected part of the unit and people who would carry it out. A discussion or submitted sketch is not approval to begin work.

    An urgent situation

    This website cannot provide emergency assistance. Follow the posted emergency arrangements and actual emergency contacts for your real building; do not wait for a response from this demonstration.
