Working here

A smaller scale, around a shared yard.

Three storeys of yellow brick enclose a planted yard. The example office suits a smaller team; the ground-floor retail shell gives a shop or showroom space to establish its own layout.

Who it could suit

For a smaller office or a non-food retail business that values a quieter shared setting. Actual use, suitability and terms need a separate review.

Arriving at the building

The shared yard leads to office access and individual ground-floor premises. Check the exact entrance, deliveries and any access needs before making a decision.

Sharing the place

The yard is a shared place, not an extension of an individual shop. Outdoor displays, deliveries and alterations would need a separate conversation.
