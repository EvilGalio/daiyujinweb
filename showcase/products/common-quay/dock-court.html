Working here

Old brick. New working days.

A retained four-storey brick block brings offices above small shops into one shared court. Separate retail entrances keep the daily rhythm of the ground floor distinct from the workplaces above.

Who it could suit

For a small office team looking for a defined layout, or a non-food shop prepared to plan its own interior. Actual use, suitability and terms need a separate review.

Arriving at the building

Retail units have their own ground-floor doors. Office arrivals use the shared stair entrance off the court. Confirm any step-free route and lift requirements during a real site review.

Sharing the place

Keep the passage through the courtyard clear. Large deliveries would need to be coordinated around the shared route and individual shop entrances.
