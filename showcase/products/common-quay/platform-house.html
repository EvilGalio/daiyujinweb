Platform House

A clear front door for a growing business.

Working here

A clear front door for a growing business.

A five-storey pale-concrete and metal block opens onto a broad forecourt. Shared reception serves the offices, while the ground floor gives retail and showroom uses their own frontage.

Who it could suit

For a larger office team comparing a fitted layout, or a showroom looking for an existing retail arrangement. Actual use, suitability and terms need a separate review.

Arriving at the building

Office visitors arrive through the shared reception. The ground-floor sample retail unit has a separate front door. Visitor hours and access arrangements would be agreed with the operator.

Sharing the place

Reception and the forecourt serve more than one occupier. Discuss visitor arrivals, larger deliveries and signage before finalising an operational plan.
