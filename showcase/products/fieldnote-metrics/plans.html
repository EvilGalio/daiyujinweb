export you have.

A sample column map tells us more than a long feature checklist. Confirm the reporting week, store and SKU identifiers, product categories, units, net sales and closing stock.

Check the CSV requirements↗
What if we have fewer than three or more than twenty stores?

The standard concept plans are designed for 3–20 stores. Prepare a walkthrough outline to identify the reporting need; a separate scope discussion would be required outside that range.

Does Fieldnote replace our POS?

No. The offer starts with a structured CSV export. It does not provide POS hardware, process transactions or promise a ready-made connector to every system.

Can I upload our actual sales file here?

No. This website accepts no files. Explore the synthetic dataset and use its schema to prepare a future discussion without sharing real business or personal data.

Are there forecast or revenue guarantees?

No. The report summarises source data. Its stock review rule is a prompt for a human decision, not a demand forecast or a promised business outcome.
