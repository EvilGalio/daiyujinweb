Useful reports need visible definitions. Here is what the sample contains, how its totals work and where this demonstration stops.

ELM LANE STORES

A small, open sample.

36 synthetic rows.
3 fictional stores.
6 products across 3 categories.
2 complete sample weeks.

Download all sample rows ↓

A CSV file with both weeks. No real customer or employee data.

The CSV shape.

Each row represents one product at one store during one week. Stable identifiers make it possible to group the same products and locations without guessing from names.

FieldMeaning in the sample
row_idA unique source identifier, 001–036, used for inspection.
week_startMonday date: 2026-08-24 or 2026-08-31. Includes that Monday through Sunday.
storejunction, riverside or market. All three are fictional Elm Lane locations.
sku / productStable product code and readable name. Two products per category.
categoryhome, pantry or stationery. The colour key follows these same categories.
units_soldWhole units sold in the selected week.
net_sales_cadSample pre-tax sales in CAD. This dataset has no discounts or returns.
closing_unitsUnits on hand at the end of the same week; not an inventory movement history.

For a future trial, returns, discounts, tax, missing stock and category changes would need agreed mapping rules. This demo does not import or validate real files.

No mystery in the metrics.

Net sales
Sum of net_sales_cad for rows matching the selected week, store and category.
Weekly change
(Selected sales − previous-week sales) ÷ previous-week sales × 100. The oldest sample week has no earlier comparison.
Sales mix
A category's selected sales divided by total selected sales. Charts show labelled amounts as well as colour.
Review flag
One store/SKU row where closing_units is less than units_sold for that week. Counted per row, not per unique product.
Coverage
Closing units ÷ units sold in the chosen week. A historical ratio in weeks; not a forecast, lead-time calculation or order instruction.

Store comparisons use raw totals. They are not adjusted for floor area, hours or promotions and do not rank employee performance.

What happens to your data here?

  • The reports read a finite local synthetic dataset served with this website. There are no live sales connections.
  • Filters and row selections remain in current-page memory. Public query presets may choose a sample view; Reset removes those presets.
  • The walkthrough planner asks for a store count, report focus and CSV readiness. It does not ask for names, emails or real sales information.
  • No answer is submitted, stored in browser storage or sent to an external service. Downloads create a file locally on your device.
  • No authentication, payment, email delivery, account infrastructure or production security certification is represented as implemented.

ABOUT FIELDNOTE

Built around
the weekly conversation.

Fieldnote Metrics is a fictional Toronto software company, founded in 2023 with a 14-person team. Its focus is independent retail chains in Canada and the US with 3–20 stores.

The product idea joins retail workflow, data mapping and interface design: make the first useful report understandable before asking a small team to maintain a large reporting system.

No real customers, reviews, funding claims or compliance credentials are used in this concept. The website demonstrates a complete product story and working local reporting experience.
