THE REPORTS

One week.
Three useful conversations.

The same source rows support three ways to read the business. Start broad, look at the shelves, then bring the store teams into the conversation.

01 Monday Brief02 Category Review03 Store Compare

01 / MONDAY BRIEF

What changed
this week?

A shared opening for the weekly trading conversation.

See net sales, units sold, the change from the previous sample week and stock rows to review. Category totals add context to the headline.

Inputs
Weekly store/SKU sales and closing stock, with a consistent week boundary.
Useful next question
Which category or store explains the difference, and what happened locally?
Keep in mind
The report shows what is in the source. It cannot explain promotions, closures or weather on its own.

02 / CATEGORY REVIEW

What deserves
a closer look?

Move from the category mix to the products behind it.

Compare category sales, then inspect the store/SKU rows. A simple stock flag highlights closing units below the units sold in the selected week.

Inputs
Product category, units sold and closing stock for the same period and store.
Useful next question
Is the low closing stock expected, and what has already been ordered?
Keep in mind
The flag and historical coverage ratio do not forecast demand or place replenishment orders.

03 / STORE COMPARE

Where is
the difference?

Same dates. Same definitions. Room for local context.

Put store sales beside one another, then narrow the comparison to a category. Review totals and supporting rows without switching datasets.

Inputs
Consistent store IDs and one selected week, category and currency.
Useful next question
What do the opening hours, floor space and local activities tell us?
Keep in mind
This view does not normalise for size or trading hours, and is not an employee performance ranking.
