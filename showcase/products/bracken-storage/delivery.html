Delivery & aftercare

From the first question
to the next service visit.

A considered storage project needs more than equipment procurement. We coordinate the people, decisions and records that carry the project from assessment through handover.

01

Put the site brief on paper

Define the main objective, the building’s working pattern and the information already available. Identify the site owner and those who can make decisions.

You leave with: a list of facts, open questions and named responsibilities.
02

Agree the assessment

Set a scope for reviewing demand, generation where relevant, equipment options and site constraints. Decide which specialist inputs are needed before committing to design.

You leave with: assessment findings, assumptions and a decision about the next stage.
03

Coordinate design and installation

Bring the equipment supplier, electrical contractor and site team around one agreed sequence. Review access, interfaces and changes with the parties responsible for them.

You leave with: a coordinated delivery scope and an agreed record of changes.
04

Make handover practical

Bring together the specified equipment information, operating documentation and records supplied by the delivery parties. Agree escalation contacts and the aftercare scope.

You leave with: the records and responsibilities your facilities team needs.
05

Keep care in the plan

Define maintenance tasks and review intervals with the relevant specialists and manufacturer information. Revisit the agreed operating purpose when the building’s use changes.

You leave with: an aftercare plan with clear ownership and service boundaries.
The project boundary

Coordination with
named ownership.

We bring the scope together. Equipment suppliers, contractors and the site team retain the work and responsibilities assigned to them in the project contract.

Bracken is not a battery manufacturer, electricity retailer or network operator. This website does not approve a site, grant a connection or replace specialist design.

Clear roles, from the start.

  1. You / the site owner

    Access, available building and supply information, and the people responsible for decisions.

  2. Bracken / project coordination

    The agreed assessment scope, design interfaces, delivery sequence and handover record.

  3. Specialists / defined work

    Equipment information, electrical work and other responsibilities assigned in the project contract.

After handover

Keep the next chapter clear.

Know the equipment.

Keep the supplied equipment record, manufacturer information and maintenance responsibilities accessible to the facilities team.

Know who to contact.

Agree the escalation route and service scope. Response times and remote services, if any, belong in that agreement rather than a general website promise.

Know what has changed.

New machinery, different tenants or revised working hours can change the operating brief. Record those changes before assuming the original plan still fits.
