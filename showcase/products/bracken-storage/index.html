From assessment to aftercare

Clear roles, from the start.

  1. You / the site owner

    Access, available building and supply information, and the people responsible for decisions.

  2. Bracken / project coordination

    The agreed assessment scope, design interfaces, delivery sequence and handover record.

  3. Specialists / defined work

    Equipment information, electrical work and other responsibilities assigned in the project contract.
