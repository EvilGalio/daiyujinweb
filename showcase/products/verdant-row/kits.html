Kits / Equipment and a field handover

A monitoring setup
with a defined scope.

Three configurations give a practical starting point. Hardware counts are provisional until placement, connectivity, power and the people reviewing the data have been considered.

The components

Simple equipment.
Specific responsibilities.

Sensor nodes and probe assemblies collect the field record. A gateway is the communications link to discuss during the site check. The grower and an appointed adviser retain the agronomic decisions.

Concept hardware shown for illustration. Package quantities below are complete scoping configurations; no radio range, battery life, accuracy or enclosure certification is claimed.

Orchard Starter

A small monitoring setup for a grower establishing a first field record.

Start with one defined block and an owner who will review the readings.

Sensor nodes
2
Probe assemblies
2
Initial gateways
1

Included in the scope

  • Two sensor nodes with two probe assemblies.
  • One gateway provision and a scoped placement plan.
  • An initial data-quality review and owner handover.

What gets handed over

A grower-facing record of node identities, locations to confirm and how missing readings will be followed up.

Positions, depths, power and gateway requirements are confirmed with the field team and the grower’s adviser. This is not a coverage-per-acre rule or an agronomic placement recommendation.

Prices, communications service and seasonal support are agreed against the actual scope. No subscription, payment or order is created here.

What these kits do not do

They do not operate valves, apply irrigation schedules or make crop decisions automatically.

They do not guarantee yield, water savings, continuous connectivity or a particular battery life.

They do not replace a site review or the interpretation of an appropriate agronomy adviser.
