In the field / Before the first useful record

Place the equipment.
Keep the context.

A block name and a reading are only part of a field record. Our setup process keeps the location, connection and review owner with the data.

  1. Define the monitoring question.

    List the blocks, the orchard context and who will review the record. The grower and an appointed adviser decide what needs to be understood.

  2. Agree the field plan.

    Review potential node positions, installation arrangements, gateway needs and power with the field team. Positions and depths are site-specific decisions, not settings from this website.

  3. Check the communications route.

    Establish how readings would leave the field and how a gap would be noticed. A spot signal observation is not a promise of continuous coverage.

  4. Review the first record.

    Confirm node identities, timestamps and missing-data handling. A data-quality review asks whether the record is present and identifiable; it does not prescribe irrigation.

  5. Hand over the review routine.

    Name the people following up equipment questions and interpreting the field context. Agree any continuing seasonal support separately.

Who owns which question?

Monitoring supports
an existing judgement.

The grower

Defines the blocks, agrees access and keeps the operating context with the record. The grower remains responsible for orchard decisions.

The field team

Scopes equipment placement and communication checks, documents node identities and follows up the agreed hardware or record issues.

The agronomy partner

Interprets readings alongside field observations and crop knowledge. Appropriate agronomic advice is separate from this concept website.

Soil-moisture information needs field observations and crop/site context. The UC Agriculture and Natural Resources background page explains these general considerations; it does not validate the fictional equipment or sample data here.

A finite first
service area.

Our primary field area is Fresno, Madera, Kings and Tulare counties. Work outside it needs a separate service discussion. Access dates, communications and seasonal support are agreed with the actual scope.

What is not
an installation approval.

A kit choice, local enquiry or sample reading does not confirm site suitability, sensor accuracy, a communication network, battery duration or permission to operate equipment.
