Example 01 / Singapore–Malaysia

A distributor needs
a clearer role.

Explore this enquiry type↗

The commercial situation

A Singapore industrial spares business is considering a Malaysia distributor. The product and sales teams have different assumptions about customer support.

The next decision
Define who would own customer communication, after-sales support and returns before asking advisers to review an agreement.
Useful work product
A product/channel outline, commercial responsibilities matrix and open-decision log. Each unresolved point is assigned an operating owner.
Still unresolved
Does the proposed distributor only sell the parts, or also coordinate support? The commercial team must settle the intention; the website does not decide it.
Specialist boundary
Separate qualified advisers would scope Singapore and Malaysia-specific questions. No clauses, enforceability opinion or transaction result are supplied.
Example 02 / Singapore–Indonesia

A service handover
with an owner.

Explore this enquiry type↗

The commercial situation

A Singapore software provider plans to work with an Indonesia support participant. The teams need a shared description of what happens after a customer request arrives.

The next decision
Separate the provider’s deliverables, the local support role and the customer’s acceptance questions.
Useful work product
A service responsibility grid, handover sequence and escalation question list. The scope distinguishes a commercial decision from an item for document review.
Still unresolved
Who confirms that a handover is complete, and who owns an unresolved request? The example records those questions without inventing agreed service levels.
Specialist boundary
Appointed advisers would determine their own local review scope and document needs. No contract interpretation, data-law conclusion or actual service performance is claimed.
Example 03 / Singapore–Vietnam

An entry plan
with fewer assumptions.

Explore this enquiry type↗

The commercial situation

A Singapore packaging supplier is exploring work with a Vietnam-based operating participant. The activity is still being defined, and the team has not appointed local reviewers.

The next decision
Clarify the intended activity and which unanswered questions must be considered before an implementation plan.
Useful work product
A commercial issue map, dependencies list and referral brief grouped by legal, tax and product/regulatory questions where relevant.
Still unresolved
Which part of the proposed activity belongs to which operating participant? The first task is to state the plan clearly enough for each specialist to scope their work.
Specialist boundary
No recommendation of entity structure, licence assessment, tax position or permission to trade. No market entry or referral is actually completed.
