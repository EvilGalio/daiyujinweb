Example commercial scoping note - fictional matter
Not legal advice, a contract, a real client engagement or a confirmed service.

MATTER A / INDUSTRIAL SPARES DISTRIBUTION
Singapore-based product owner considering a Malaysia distributor.
Decision: agree which commercial roles need defining before document review.

IN SCOPE FOR COMMERCIAL COORDINATION
1. Record proposed product groups and customer channels.
2. Identify an owner for orders, customer contact, after-sales and returns.
3. Distinguish agreed intentions from unresolved territory assumptions.
4. Prepare questions for separately appointed qualified advisers.

RESPONSIBILITY GRID
Product owner: clarify intended product scope and service limits.
Proposed distributor: clarify channel and local operating assumptions.
Commercial coordinator: maintain the decision log and review handover.
Separate local advisers: accept their own scope for jurisdiction-specific questions.

OPEN QUESTIONS
Which products and channels are proposed?
Who owns after-sales communication and the returns handover?
Which Singapore and Malaysia reviewers will address local questions?
Which decisions remain commercial choices rather than settled terms?

PROPOSED OUTPUT
A responsibilities matrix, open-decision log and question pack.
There is no model agreement, recommended clause or enforceability conclusion.
No real documents or party names are included in this example.

ENGAGEMENT BOUNDARIES
Subject and jurisdiction fit must be checked.
Responsible providers, appropriate checks, written scope, exclusions and fees
must be agreed outside this demonstration before any real work.
