Engagement / Before work begins

Agree the work
before the handover.

A useful first conversation does not need confidential material. It needs a clear topic, the countries involved and the decision you are trying to make.

  1. Establish the subject fit.

    Start with the category of commercial work and the jurisdictions involved. Separate distributor coordination, service alignment or an early entry question from matters outside this practice.

  2. Identify the responsible providers.

    Clarify what a commercial coordinator would do, what an existing adviser already owns and which questions need another specialist. Appropriate provider checks take place before a real engagement.

  3. Define outputs and exclusions.

    Agree the intended work product, assumptions, review owners, dependencies and the fee in writing. There is no public fee or automatic retainer offered on this website.

  4. Agree the document channel.

    Only after the relevant provider has accepted its scope should a secure handover be arranged with that provider. This website has no upload, secure portal or confidential messaging channel.

Scope and confidentiality

Some boundaries are best
made explicit at the start.

Coordination is a defined role.

Our three workstreams organise commercial questions and responsibilities. They do not replace advice from an appropriately appointed provider on a jurisdiction’s law, tax or regulated activities.

Not every matter belongs here.

Disputes, litigation and personal immigration are outside this practice. Unknown jurisdictions need a boundary discussion first. An urgent selection in the planner does not create a response commitment or extend a deadline.

A website is not an engagement.

This is a fictional firm. No real professional relationship, conflict check, referral or contract results from browsing, selecting options or downloading a note. Do not share names, documents or confidential circumstances here.

For a real Singapore provider search: the Ministry of Law’s public resource page links the LSRA directory. Use official resources and confirm the appropriate provider for your own circumstances.
