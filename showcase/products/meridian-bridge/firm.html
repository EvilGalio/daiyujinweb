The firm / Singapore / Established 2020

Close to the question.
Clear about the role.

Meridian Bridge is a 14-person commercial advisory team built around a defined brief, a shared decision log and deliberate handovers.

For founders and operating teams

A focused practice
with a regional perspective.

Our work is shaped for Singapore-based businesses whose operating teams need to coordinate a distributor, a service arrangement or an early entry plan. The typical planning context is an organisation of 10–250 people with a specific commercial decision ahead.

We stay close to that decision. A responsibilities matrix or a well-prepared question pack can make the next specialist conversation more focused.

Malaysia, Indonesia and Vietnam are the regional settings in our examples. Each project still needs its own scope and its own appropriate local review owners.
