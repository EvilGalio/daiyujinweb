SIGNAL FOLD PICTURES / FICTIONAL CONCEPT TREATMENT
Maker at Work
Brand documentary; 01:30 proposed length; 2 proposed shoot days

Objective: Let a viewer understand the care, repetition and judgement behind a screen print before asking them to value the finished object.
Audience: People choosing objects made by a small independent workshop.
Story: We arrive before an introduction. A frame is set, paper is aligned and the first pull of ink gives the day its rhythm. Only after watching the work do we meet the maker. The closing image returns to the print, now carrying the decisions we have seen.
Approach: Stay close without directing every gesture. One conversation provides context; working sounds give the sequence its structure. The workshop remains a working place, not a dressed showroom.
