Form, made useful

A name about form. A fixed bench.

A softly eased 30mm seat meets two A-profile end supports and a modest lengthwise stretcher. The name comes from those folded-looking planes. It is a fixed bench, with no hinges, folding action or height adjustment.

Check the space beside the table or in the hallway, and leave a clear circulation route. Seating capacity and suitability should be discussed for the actual household; no tested load rating is published.
