Room for everyday things

Lowline shelf

Two open bays, a low horizon and a place for books and the things that make a room yours. Lowline gives storage a quiet, useful shape.

Form, made useful

Two open bays. Two useful widths.

Three oak boards meet two full-height slab sides. The back stays open and the middle shelf stays fixed: a clear construction with nothing to conceal. A bowl, a row of books or a space deliberately left empty all belong here.

Room placement and any restraint or wall-fixing requirements must be reviewed before use. Wall types and fixing suitability vary. No load capacity or tip-resistance certification is claimed by this concept.

Living with it

A little attention,
over time.
