A place to gather

Trest table

A broad oak surface, a pair of grounded supports and room for the day to unfold. Trest makes a calm centre for eating, working and being together.

Form, made useful

Two lengths. A grounded outline.

The shape begins with two splayed trestle ends, joined by a low lengthwise stretcher. Above them, a solid oak top has a gently eased edge. There is enough definition to give the piece character, without asking the rest of the room to follow it.

Allow for your actual chairs, movement around the table and the route into the room. The listed size is the table footprint, not a recommendation for the size of a dining area.
