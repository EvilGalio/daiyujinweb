Begin with a useful form.

Choose a table, bench or shelf and the size that fits your intention. Our range has defined dimensions; it is not a promise of any shape or size.

02 / THE DETAILS

Agree what will be made.

Review a physical finish sample, the room and access route. A written quotation and specification settle the scope before an order.

Production and delivery are agreed after approval. Eight to twelve weeks is a planning assumption, not a reserved slot or guaranteed arrival date.
