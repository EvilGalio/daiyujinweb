Useful before you enquire

A few practical things.

Can I change the dimensions?

This collection has the two listed sizes per piece. Unlimited bespoke fabrication, extensions and changes to the underlying structure are outside its scope. Start with the size closest to your actual room and discuss suitability before an order.

Where do you work with customers?

Our intended service region is Belgium and the Netherlands. Other destinations need a separate scope decision. The website does not calculate shipping, confirm delivery access or reserve a delivery date.

How long does making take?

Eight to twelve weeks after final written approval is a planning assumption only. A real schedule depends on the specification and an available production slot, agreed before the order. This website has no live workshop schedule.

Do you work with interior designers?

Yes, for private residential projects within the same collection and region. The homeowner's requirements, dimensions, finish and access route still need written confirmation. Commercial furniture certification and fit-out contracts are outside this collection.

What should I prepare?

Choose a piece and size, consider both finishes, and measure the room and access route. The local enquiry tool gives you a structured note to keep. Do not submit personal details, addresses or site photographs in this concept website.
