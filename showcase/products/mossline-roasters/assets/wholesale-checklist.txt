MOSSLINE ROASTERS
A first cafe conversation

Fictional roastery / concept document. This file does not send an enquiry or book a tasting.

Prepare a short outline of:
1. Your cafe's region and whether it is open, changing coffee or preparing to launch.
2. Approximate weekly coffee use and your busiest service periods.
3. Your milk/black/filter/decaf menu and preferred flavour profile.
4. Current espresso machine, grinder and the team using them.
5. Ordering rhythm, storage space and supply questions to resolve.
6. Tasting, initial recipe guidance and ongoing support you would like to discuss.

Potential scope: coffee supply, a directly agreed tasting, opening recipe notes and a defined check-in rhythm for independent Wellington-region cafes.

Agree in writing before starting: supply terms, prices, responsibilities, contact rhythm, support boundaries and any separate equipment arrangement.

No minimum volume, wholesale rate, machine lease, installation, emergency repair, national technician coverage or franchise is included by this outline.
