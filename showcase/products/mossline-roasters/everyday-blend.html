A bit about this coffee

For a familiar morning cup, with or without milk.

Our steady house blend is built around a comfortable, rounded cup. It is a good starting point for a home espresso machine, a shared plunger or someone who likes their coffee with milk.

Tasting notes describe the cup; they are not added ingredients.

  • Whole beanFor grinding at home before you brew.
  • Espresso (fine)A fine starting grind for espresso equipment.
  • Filter (medium)A medium grind for paper-filter brewers.
  • Plunger (coarse)A coarser grind for immersion brewing.

A place to start

Brew it your way.

Start with 18g in and 36g out on an espresso machine. Adjust the grind for your own basket and taste; one fixed pre-ground setting cannot suit every machine.

Equipment, water and preference all matter. Keep a note of what tastes good, then adjust one thing at a time.
