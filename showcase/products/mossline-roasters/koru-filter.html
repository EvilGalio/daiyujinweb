A bit about this coffee

For paper-filter brewers who enjoy fruit alongside sweetness.

Koru is the one to brew when you have a few minutes to yourself. Its lighter profile puts the detail first. Try it black through a paper filter, then decide whether a splash of milk belongs in your cup.
