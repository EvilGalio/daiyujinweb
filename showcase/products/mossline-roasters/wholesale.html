What the relationship can cover

A useful scope,
agreed together.

House coffee supply, a tasting to choose the profile, opening recipe notes and an agreed check-in rhythm. Everyday Blend is our first conversation for milk-led menus; Koru and Evening may fit a filter or decaf offer.

We discuss expected usage, ordering rhythm, equipment and what your team needs before proposing terms. No minimum volume, wholesale price or machine package is promised on this site.

We do not operate a franchise, provide round-the-clock equipment repair or promise technician coverage outside our agreed local scope.

Location, opening stage, approximate weekly coffee use, busiest periods and the equipment you already have. An estimate is useful when you are starting out.

A few first questions.

Can you supply cafes outside Wellington?

Our active relationship model focuses on the Wellington region. A different location would need a separate conversation about supply and support; national service is not assumed.

Do you supply or service espresso machines?

Equipment needs can be discussed, but no machine, lease, installation or repair service is included by default. The chosen equipment provider and support scope would be identified separately.
