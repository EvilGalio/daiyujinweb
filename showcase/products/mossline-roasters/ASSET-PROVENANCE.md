# Mossline Roasters asset provenance

All company identity, products, prices and product stories are original fictional project content. This concept does not claim farm ownership, real producer relationships, certification, awards or customer endorsements.

## Original generated imagery

The built-in OpenAI image generation tool created four original images on 2026-09-11, one call per image, with no reference images. Every resulting PNG was opened with view_image before use. Each selected image was copied into this company directory. Full PNG originals are retained; prepare-assets.mjs recreates 768/1536 pixel WebP derivatives with existing Sharp, quality 85, without upscaling. No stock image was downloaded and no external stock licence or exclusivity is claimed.

The three product pictures illustrate the 250g concept packs. Product captions and the bag selector state that size; 1kg selections are separate stated options, not a claim that a 250g photograph changes size. The tasting-counter scene is fictional, with no real cafe represented. The footer identifies the original generated imagery.

## Actual references

- [Flight Coffee](https://flightcoffee.co.nz/), read 2026-09-11: catalogue grouping and the separation of local wholesale from home-coffee retail informed the information architecture.
- [Coffee Supreme](https://coffeesupreme.com/), read and visually inspected with BrowserUse 2026-09-11: readable retail navigation, a product-led catalogue and everyday coffee language informed the tone. Its catalogue, prices, imagery, subscriptions and actual claims were not copied.

## Brewing source

[Hario UK basic V60 guide](https://www.hario.co.uk/pages/brew-guides-v60-basic), read 2026-09-11, supports the paper-filter starting method and approximate coffee/water amounts. [Hario USA recipes](https://www.hario-usa.com/pages/recipes-and-guides) was also checked when an older Hario URL failed. Our wording is independently written and identifies recipes as starting points, with equipment instructions taking precedence. No brewer endorsement, fixed performance, caffeine-free, health or decaffeination-process claim is made. Espresso/plunger quantities are the company's illustrative starting recipes rather than universal standards.

The downloadable wholesale checklist is an original plain-text document, not a real supplier agreement.
