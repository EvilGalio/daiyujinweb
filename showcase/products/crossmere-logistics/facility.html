01 / 1,200 m²

Receiving: Check the arrival before it becomes stock.

Match the arrival outline, count the units and record visible discrepancies for the stock owner. A receipt is not a silent acceptance of an unexplained difference.

Handover: Arrival outline, pallet identifiers, carton counts and a discrepancy contact.

02 / 4,800 m²

Storage: Give each agreed stock unit a place.

Locate identified pallets according to the agreed stock profile. Product dimensions, weight and handling needs determine the discussion; floor area alone does not establish pallet capacity.

Handover: Agreed stock master, unit dimensions and weights, and release authority.

03 / 1,000 m²

Pick & pack: Turn an order into a defined handover.

Select the agreed stock lines, follow the packing instructions and flag any mismatch before a carton is released. This is a concept workflow, not a live order system.

Handover: Sample order, SKU-to-pack rules, label requirements and exception owner.

04 / 1,000 m²

Dispatch: Stage the release for the next owner.

Group the prepared units by release instruction and document the carrier handover. Transport is separately arranged by the customer; no delivery time is guaranteed here.

Handover: Release instruction, collection arrangement and carrier handover record.
