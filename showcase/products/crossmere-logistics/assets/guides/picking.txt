Pick & Pack
Carton-level order preparation for defined stock lines, with agreed picking and pack rules.

Fit: Small B2B orders that combine stock lines and need a consistent carton handover.
Inputs: SKU and variant labels, order examples, packing instructions and the customer’s carrier requirements.
Output: A documented pick/pack method, sample order walkthrough and dispatch handover checklist.
Starting check: Agree the SKU-to-carton rules and how order discrepancies are returned for a decision.

Customer owns goods classification, stock master and carrier arrangement. No quote, capacity confirmation, shipment, customs or dangerous-goods service. Nothing is sent through this website.
