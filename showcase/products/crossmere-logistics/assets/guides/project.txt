Project Stock
Separately identified campaign stock, staged release batches and an organised close-out.

Fit: A defined rollout or campaign with stock reserved by project and release batches agreed in advance.
Inputs: A project stock list, release sequence, batch owners and a plan for residual stock.
Output: A project stock index, batch release checklist and campaign close-out record.
Starting check: Assign each release batch an owner and agree the treatment of unallocated stock.

Customer owns goods classification, stock master and carrier arrangement. No quote, capacity confirmation, shipment, customs or dangerous-goods service. Nothing is sent through this website.
