Warehouse services

What comes in.
What needs to go out.

Three defined ways to handle regional B2B stock. Goods, unit sizes, release patterns and responsibilities are agreed before a warehouse scope.

01 / warehouse service

Storage Start

Pallet receiving, agreed storage locations and release instructions for regular B2B inventory.

Discuss Storage StartScope guide (TXT)

A good starting fit

Packaged stock held in pallet quantities, with a defined owner and a predictable release pattern.

What you provide

A stock master, pallet dimensions and weights, arrival outline and authorised release contacts.

The proposed output

An opening stock record, agreed location method and a pallet release handover.

Confirm pallet identity and receiving discrepancies before the starting stock record is agreed.

02 / warehouse service

Pick & Pack

Carton-level order preparation for defined stock lines, with agreed picking and pack rules.

Discuss Pick & PackScope guide (TXT)

A good starting fit

Small B2B orders that combine stock lines and need a consistent carton handover.

What you provide

SKU and variant labels, order examples, packing instructions and the customer’s carrier requirements.

The proposed output

A documented pick/pack method, sample order walkthrough and dispatch handover checklist.

Agree the SKU-to-carton rules and how order discrepancies are returned for a decision.

03 / warehouse service

Project Stock

Separately identified campaign stock, staged release batches and an organised close-out.

Discuss Project StockScope guide (TXT)

A good starting fit

A defined rollout or campaign with stock reserved by project and release batches agreed in advance.

What you provide

A project stock list, release sequence, batch owners and a plan for residual stock.

The proposed output

A project stock index, batch release checklist and campaign close-out record.

Assign each release batch an owner and agree the treatment of unallocated stock.
