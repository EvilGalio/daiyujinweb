The record link shows the individual readings and the exact arithmetic behind the current displayed mean.

The calculation is explicit.

Mean = (reading 1 + reading 2 + reading 3) / 3. The optional adjusted view subtracts 50, the raw BLK mean, from every reading and every mean. It does not change the source values.

Adjusted mean = raw mean − 50

Keep the limitation attached.

The reference record is an illustration, not a validated control. These numbers describe no real assay, biological effect or patient. The CSV identifies the active calculation and retains the raw values beside the displayed ones.
