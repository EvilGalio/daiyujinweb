HW-P07 / Pilot Plate

Making the background visible.

Read the service scope ↗

The starting question

Could a first plate distinguish the proposed research signal from its buffer background?

Information supplied

A non-clinical buffer description, a proposed readout and a short set of prior observations.

How the work was framed

The project was scoped around the measurement question and the records needed to revisit it. A labelled example output made the background contribution visible beside the individual readings.

The handover

Feasibility note, raw-value file, calculation sheet and a list of assumptions to revisit.

The study refined the question. It did not prove target binding, establish a concentration or validate a method.

HW-T12 / Transfer Review

Finding the missing setting.

Read the service scope ↗

The starting question

What was missing between a written plate method and the receiving team's example output?

Information supplied

A source method, two instrument-setting summaries and an illustrative calculation workbook.

How the work was framed

A line-by-line handover comparison identified an undocumented calculation convention. That convention remained an open item rather than being silently guessed.

The handover

Method comparison, version list and unresolved-setting register.

No claim of method equivalence was made. A further agreed study would be needed before any such conclusion.

HW-R04 / Research Batch

Keeping the exception in the record.

Read the service scope ↗

The starting question

How should a small repeat-readout project preserve an unusual record during handover?

Information supplied

An agreed research method, coded item list and the intended report structure.

How the work was framed

The handover linked each record to its raw values and calculation version. One record was retained with an explicit note, so the receiving team could see both the value and the question around it.

The handover

Run record, traceable raw file, calculation notes and exception register.

The record was not silently removed or labelled clinically meaningful. The example demonstrates reporting decisions, not a biological finding.
