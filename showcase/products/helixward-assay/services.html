Each engagement starts with a bounded question. These three offers explain what to bring, what the handover contains and what it cannot establish.

Research-only, non-clinical projects. Scope, fees, schedule and any further work would be agreed individually. This website does not book lab capacity or issue quotations.

01 / A question to explore

Pilot Plate

A small feasibility study around one plate-based research readout.

What you bring

  • The research question and the decision it should inform
  • A description of the material and the proposed readout
  • Any existing observations, including what did not work

What you receive

  • A scoped feasibility note
  • Labelled raw output and a calculation sheet
  • An open-questions register for the next discussion

Keep the boundary clear. A feasibility study can expose a limitation. It does not promise a working assay, a biological conclusion or a validated method.

02 / A method to hand over

Transfer Review

A documented comparison between the method you have and the handover you need.

What you bring

  • The current written method and its version
  • Instrument settings and the calculation convention
  • A labelled example output with known limitations

What you receive

  • A source-to-receiving-method comparison
  • A record of missing settings and assumptions
  • A proposed discussion sequence before any lab work

Keep the boundary clear. A document review is not proof that two instruments or laboratories produce equivalent results. Any further work needs a separately agreed scope.

03 / A defined readout to repeat

Research Batch

A bounded set of research items, run against a method already agreed for the project.

What you bring

  • The agreed method and calculation version
  • A coded item manifest with no personal information
  • The reporting structure and exception rules

What you receive

  • A labelled run record and raw values
  • A calculation sheet linked to the source records
  • An exception register and project handover note

Keep the boundary clear. Repeated readings do not establish clinical meaning, product performance or regulatory suitability. Exceptions remain visible in the handover.
