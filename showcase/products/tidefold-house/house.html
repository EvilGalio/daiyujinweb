Our house & arrival

Small by choice.
Open at heart.

An independent house, eighteen rooms and a welcome that leaves you space to settle in. Our home is near Vila Nova de Milfontes, on the Alentejo coast of Portugal.

The house since 2019

Built around
coming together.

The courtyard came first: a place to eat breakfast, ask a question and come back to at the end of the day. The house opened in 2019 with eighteen rooms and a simple idea of hospitality.

Fourteen year-round team members look after the rooms, kitchen and welcome, with seasonal help in busier months. We are a small house for couples, friends and families, rather than a large resort or an events venue.

We offer a room, breakfast, a few house suppers and local conversation. There is no spa programme, wedding package or list of experiences you are expected to complete.

The practical parts

Arrive, then
make yourself at home.

Getting here

Our house sits just outside Vila Nova de Milfontes, away from a busy resort setting. Allow time to explore the surrounding coast at your own pace. There is no hotel transfer service; plan your transport before you travel.

Your arrival

Check-in is 15:00–20:00, with check-out by 11:00. A small parking court is available at the house. Airport transfers and a 24-hour reception are not offered.

Moving around

The courtyard is cobbled. Courtyard Rooms have door thresholds; Terrace Rooms and Family Lofts require one flight of stairs. There is no lift. Consider the cobbles, thresholds and stairs when choosing a room.

Good to know

A few things
before you arrive.

What is included in the room rate?

Breakfast for every guest, a private shower room, linen and towels, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, drinking-water refill and use of the shared courtyard. The displayed fixed rates are illustrative. Supper and drinks are not included.

Can we stay as a family?

Yes, within each room’s published occupancy. The Family Loft has one queen bed and two separate single daybeds for up to four guests. Its sleeping area is open-plan. Count every adult and child in the planner; there are no extra cots or beds.

Is there a pool, spa or private beach?

No. Tidefold is a small guesthouse with a courtyard and a house kitchen. Nearby scenery and day-out ideas are separate from hotel facilities.

Can we bring a pet?

We do not accommodate pets. This keeps the small shared spaces and rooms comfortable for guests with different needs.

How do stay plans and cancellations work?

The planner makes a local, illustrative summary only. It does not reserve a room, take money or create a booking that needs cancelling. Refresh or use Reset to clear it. Real availability, payment and cancellation terms would need to come from an operating accommodation provider.

Can I contact or book this hotel?

Tidefold House is a fictional company created as a complete website concept. It has no operating reception or booking address. No email, phone number or contact form is presented as a real hotel contact. You can explore the room choices and build a stay summary without entering personal information.
