Around the house

Out for the day.
Home by supper.

Around Vila Nova de Milfontes, the day can be as full or as quiet as you like. Start with one idea, leave a little space and come home when you are ready.

Choose the shape of your day

A little further,
or not far at all.

For a morning outside

Follow the water.

Head towards town and choose a short riverside wander, or set aside more time for the coast. Take water and a layer, and choose a route that suits the whole group.

A day for walking shoes and a light layer. Check current access and conditions locally before choosing a coastal route.

For a change of pace

Take the long lunch.

Browse town at your own speed and find somewhere for lunch. Leave enough of the afternoon for one more coffee or the book you brought along.

A day at your own pace. Shops and restaurants in town are independent of the house; check their current opening hours before setting out.

For no reason at all

Stay in the shade.

Find a cane chair in the courtyard, refill your water and let the afternoon pass. The most useful thing on a short break can be an empty page in the plan.

At the house. The courtyard is shared with other guests; there is no pool or private beach.
