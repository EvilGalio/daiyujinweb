Year-end Ready
An organised handover, with fewer loose ends.

Suitable for: For owners preparing a records handover to an independently appointed statutory adviser.
Inputs: Period records, prior working files, an open-items list and the adviser’s requested handover format.
Outputs: An indexed records pack, reconciliation working files and a visible outstanding-information schedule.
Responsibility: Your appointed adviser decides what is required for statutory work and undertakes any filing. Ledgerwell does not replace that adviser.
Rhythm: A separately scoped preparation project; milestones follow the state of your records.

No engagement, advice, filing, messages or record upload through this website. Fees and timing require a separately agreed scope.
