Monthly Books

A dependable starting point, every month. For owners whose day-to-day records need a regular rhythm.

Discuss Monthly BooksDownload scope (TXT)

What you provide

Sales and purchase records, bank exports provided through an agreed secure route, and answers to the previous month’s open questions.

What you receive

Prepared transaction records, a reconciliation working file and an unresolved-items list.

Where responsibility sits

You confirm business purpose and supply missing evidence. Ledgerwell prepares the records; your statutory adviser retains their separate role.

02 / Monthly preparation, with a quarterly pack and discussion agenda.

Quarterly View

A little distance. A clearer conversation. For service businesses that want a quarterly management discussion alongside their monthly records.

Discuss Quarterly ViewDownload scope (TXT)

What you provide

The Monthly Books inputs, agreed reporting categories, and context for unusual receipts or payments.

What you receive

Monthly Books outputs plus a quarterly management pack, cash movement view and an owner discussion agenda.

Where responsibility sits

You agree the reporting categories and explain business changes. Reports support a discussion; they are not predictions or statutory accounts.

03 / A separately scoped preparation project; milestones follow the state of your records.

Year-end Ready

An organised handover, with fewer loose ends. For owners preparing a records handover to an independently appointed statutory adviser.

Discuss Year-end ReadyDownload scope (TXT)

What you provide

Period records, prior working files, an open-items list and the adviser’s requested handover format.

What you receive

An indexed records pack, reconciliation working files and a visible outstanding-information schedule.

Where responsibility sits

Your appointed adviser decides what is required for statutory work and undertakes any filing. Ledgerwell does not replace that adviser.
