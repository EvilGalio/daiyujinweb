How we show up

Plain language.
Room for a useful challenge.

Ask who can act.

A technically sensible recommendation still needs an owner with authority, time and context. We make that part of the discussion.

Say what is unknown.

An untested area stays untested in the report. We do not fill an evidence gap with a stronger claim.

Keep the work proportionate.

Start with the question and its boundaries. Additional systems or supplier groups need a scope decision.

Leave something usable.

A record should help the next person understand the decision, evidence and follow-up without recreating the conversation.
