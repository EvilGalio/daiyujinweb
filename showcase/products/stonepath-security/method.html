Our method

A scope you can understand.
A handover you can use.

The work starts before a review or workshop day. We agree what the engagement can answer, what evidence it will use and what your team will receive.

1

Agree the question.

Name the business question, systems or supplier set, people and deliverables. Write down what is excluded before agreeing a fixed-fee scope.

Your part: choose a sponsor who can make scope decisions.

2

Prepare the context.

Bring high-level process notes and the people who own the work. Agree how evidence will be shared and handled; keep unnecessary personal or operational data outside the conversation.

Your part: nominate a coordinator and relevant role-holders.

3

Review or rehearse.

Use the agreed materials and conversations. In a tabletop, pause at the decision points. In a review, keep each observation tied to the evidence that supports it.

Your part: correct context, challenge assumptions and name unknowns.

4

Read it together.

Walk through the register, log or map. Discuss the evidence, proposed actions and unanswered questions. A role shown in a report still needs to accept ownership.

Your part: bring the people able to authorise the next steps.

5

Hand over the next work.

Record owners, dependencies and an agreed review point. The organisation takes implementation forward. Additional assurance, testing or follow-up is separately scoped.

Your part: decide what is accepted and maintain the action record.
