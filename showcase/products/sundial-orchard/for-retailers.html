Before anything
goes in your fridge.

The actual label

Ask for a verified ingredient and nutrition panel, any relevant dietary information, barcode and production specifications. This concept has no tested labels.

The shelf plan

Confirm carton dimensions, storage conditions, shelf life and stock rotation. No shelf-life or handling guarantee is published for fictional drinks.

The commercial bits

Discuss wholesale pricing, delivery coverage, initial quantities, payment terms and returns. The AUD 24 home mixed case is not a trade price.
