Sundial Orchard is a fictional drinks company imagined in Adelaide in 2023. The starting point was three flavours with different personalities, and one straightforward format: a 250 ml sparkling drink.

We chose blood orange for its gently bitter edge, pear and ginger for a warm finish, and lemon myrtle for an aromatic citrus lift. Not thirty variations. Three choices with a reason to be here.

Our imagined team of seventeen spans recipe development, packaging and operations, and relationships with independent shops. The business is built around Australian home mixed cases, grocers and café counters.

01

Flavour comes first.

We describe what a drink might taste like, so you can choose. No wellness promise, performance pitch or supplement story is needed.

02

Keep choosing easy.

Three flavours. One can size. Any six make a home case. The same price per can lets you mix to your taste.

03

Be clear about the idea.

This whole company is a concept project. The recipes, packaging and prices demonstrate a brand and a working website; they are not products available to buy.
