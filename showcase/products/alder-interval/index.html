The way we look

Look closely.
Change what matters.

Sometimes the most useful move is a room-sized addition. Sometimes it is a low shelf, a different threshold or a garden brought into the plan. We make those decisions with the people who will live with them.

From an observation to a decision

Understand the place.

Record what is known, what is valued and which questions still need evidence.

Work through the options.

Draw a small number of purposeful alternatives and make their differences clear.

Keep the intent together.

Coordinate the agreed design with the appointed team and the people making decisions.
