Our practice / Copenhagen

Close to the place.
Close to the people.

An 11-person practice, established in 2015. We work on small civic and residential commissions in Denmark, with a continuing interest in the useful life of existing places.

We are interested in the moment when an observation becomes a useful change: a room opened to the garden, a store made into a reading place, a hall given a shared purpose.

Small enough to stay involved

A project team
you can follow.

The same project lead carries the brief through the agreed design stages. A second architect reviews the choices at key moments; a coordinator keeps drawings, questions and responsibilities visible to the wider team.

Our 11-person studio includes two directors, six project architects, two design assistants and one studio coordinator. These are the roles of the fictional practice, not a claim about real staff or professional registration.

What we bring to a commission

A measured beginning.

We begin with your intended use and the information already available. Existing drawings, a description of daily routines and a clear decision-maker are more useful than an early list of finishes.

Reuse projects ask what should remain. New homes ask how shared and private life relate. Community spaces ask how different activities can coexist.

A clear edge to our role.

We develop the brief, explore spatial options and coordinate the agreed architectural design. We do not act as the construction contractor.

Structural and services engineering, cost advice and other specialist work require separate appointments. Statutory decisions rest with the relevant authorities. No permission or technical outcome is promised by this website.

The conversations we welcome

When the question is still open.

Owners, small developers and community organisations come to us when a place needs a new use, a home needs a clearer plan or a shared room needs to work better. We agree the first piece of work before deciding the full commission.
