MEREK PACKWORKS | Link
Fictional company / concept equipment sheet

A straight gravity roller section joins a bench, sealer or dispatch point. It gives flat-bottomed cartons a defined transfer surface without adding another powered system.

Transfer: Unpowered gravity rollers
Roller bed: 1,500 × 500 mm
Adjustable height: 650–850 mm
Loaded case: Up to 20 kg per case
Base condition: Flat, dry carton
Control: No powered accumulation

Operation: The final slope, stops, joints and support positions depend on the surrounding line. Link is a transfer component, not a standalone carton buffer or a guaranteed throughput solution.
Selection limits: Suitable for flat, dry cartons up to 500 mm wide. Soft-bottom packs, uncontrolled gradients and powered accumulation are outside this standard offer.
Support: Replaceable rollers and adjustable supports; final interface and handling review sit within the installation scope.

All specifications are illustrative and require project-specific review. Machine footprint excludes access and connected equipment. No compliance or certified performance claim is made.
