FORM / E200

Original concept visual; final engineering is project-specific.

An open carton.
A practical starting point.

Turn flat carton blanks into open, bottom-taped cases beside your packing bench. The E200 is intended for regular-slotted cartons run in planned batches.

Operation
Automatic erecting; bottom tape
Carton length
200–500 mm
Carton width
150–400 mm
Carton height
120–400 mm
Planning rate
Up to 12 cases/min, subject to trial
Machine body
2,200 × 1,600 mm

How it fits your line

An operator replenishes the blank magazine and resets the guides between carton formats. Loading direction, board behaviour and tape specification are agreed using your actual samples.

Selection limits

Best suited to repeated runs of dry, regular-slotted cartons. Die-cut trays, wet board and mixed random formats need a different assessment.

Service access

Accessible tape unit, common belt wear parts and a format-setting record supplied at handover.

SEAL / S40

Original concept visual; final engineering is project-specific.

Keep the packing bench.
Finish the case.

An operator-fed top and bottom tape sealer for lines where people still place the product. Handwheel settings make the change between planned carton batches explicit.

Operation
Operator-fed top/bottom tape
Carton length
200–600 mm
Carton width
150–500 mm
Carton height
120–500 mm
Loaded case
Up to 20 kg
Planning rate
Up to 20 cases/min; operator dependent
Machine body
1,200 × 900 mm

How it fits your line

Set the width and head height for a carton batch, then feed closed cases through the side belts. Tape adhesion and carton presentation are checked together before the machine is selected.

Selection limits

A fit for a fixed case size within each run. It does not adjust itself to a stream of randomly sized boxes.

Service access

Top tape head and belt access are demonstrated to the maintenance team; wear-part references are included in the handover pack.

TRANSFER / Link

Original concept drawing; final engineering is project-specific.

The useful space
between machines.

A straight gravity roller section joins a bench, sealer or dispatch point. It gives flat-bottomed cartons a defined transfer surface without adding another powered system.

Transfer
Unpowered gravity rollers
Roller bed
1,500 × 500 mm
Adjustable height
650–850 mm
Loaded case
Up to 20 kg per case
Base condition
Flat, dry carton
Control
No powered accumulation

How it fits your line

The final slope, stops, joints and support positions depend on the surrounding line. Link is a transfer component, not a standalone carton buffer or a guaranteed throughput solution.

Selection limits

Suitable for flat, dry cartons up to 500 mm wide. Soft-bottom packs, uncontrolled gradients and powered accumulation are outside this standard offer.

Service access

Replaceable rollers and adjustable supports; final interface and handling review sit within the installation scope.
