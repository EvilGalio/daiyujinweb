Installation & support

Ready for the floor.
Ready for the next shift.

A carton machine is useful when your operators can run it, your fitters can reach it and everyone knows what was agreed.

01

Review the layout

We mark the carton flow, replenishment point, operator position and service side on your floor plan. Existing benches and upstream equipment stay in the conversation.

YOU RECEIVE
A proposed equipment arrangement and an open-items list for utilities, access and line interfaces.

02

Work with your cartons

Send representative blanks and packed cases through your agreed project contact. We review folding, tape adhesion, handling direction and the least forgiving format.

YOU RECEIVE
A sample-trial record with the formats tested, adjustment settings and remaining limitations.

03

Agree the factory trial

The run conditions are written down before acceptance: actual cartons, target rate, replenishment and changeovers. Any unresolved item remains visible before dispatch.

YOU RECEIVE
A recorded trial, agreed action list and delivery responsibilities. A catalogue rate is never the entire acceptance test.

04

Hand over the working line

In Italy we coordinate the installation directly. In neighbouring EU markets, the integration partner handles the agreed site work with our machine documentation.

YOU RECEIVE
Operating information, format records, wear-part references and an operator/maintenance walk-through within the contracted scope.

A fictional dry-goods packer near Parma needs three carton formats at a planning rate of eight cases per minute. Its team wants to keep the manual packing bench.

The proposed arrangement puts an E200 before the bench and an S40 after it, with Link towards dispatch. The illustrated equipment envelope is 6.2 × 2.0 m. Operator access, blank storage and connected services require a separate site review.

After the handover

Keep the machine identity, format record and wear-part list with your maintenance team. A useful support enquiry describes the carton, current setup, observed behaviour and which checks have already been made.

We can scope replacement wear parts, format reviews and partner visits. Availability, response time and pricing are agreed per request; the website does not schedule a service visit.

Clear responsibilities

We supply the agreed carton-handling equipment and its handover material. The site and integrator coordinate the surrounding installation, utility readiness and the final operating arrangement.

Food-contact filling, upstream production machinery and a complete factory control system are outside the Merek offer.
