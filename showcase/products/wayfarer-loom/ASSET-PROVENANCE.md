# Wayfarer Loom asset provenance

This is an original concept website for a fictional Valencia planning studio. It does not document completed client trips or a real travel operator.

## Generated regional photographs

Three original images were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-11, one call per image. No reference image was supplied and no photograph was downloaded from either inspiration site. Each generated PNG was inspected with view_image before use. These are illustrative regional scenes, not verified exact locations, venues, partner properties, accommodation conditions or documentary travel photographs. The footer and relevant image captions state that distinction.

Original PNGs are retained in assets/images. Sharp converts each to 768 and 1536 pixel WebP at quality 85 with no upscaling; prepare-assets.mjs can recreate those derivatives from the included originals. Generation tool use does not imply a third-party stock licence or exclusivity claim.

## Fonts and original graphics

The bundled SIL Open Font License 1.1 texts are retained with the local WOFF2 files at assets/fonts/SOURCE-SERIF-LICENSE.txt and assets/fonts/SPACE-GROTESK-LICENSE.txt. No external font request is made. The woven brand mark, favicon, arrows, labels, postcard composition and two-node route sketches are original SVG/HTML/CSS for this project. The route sketches are explicitly not to scale and not navigation maps.

- [Black Tomato](https://www.blacktomato.com/), read and visually inspected in BrowserUse on 2026-09-11: a clear trip-planning action alongside strong destination imagery informed the homepage emphasis. Its global luxury or concierge model is not assigned to this smaller regional studio.
