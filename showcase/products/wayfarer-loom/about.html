Wayfarer Loom is an independent team of eight planners and coordinators. We work on private trips within Spain and Portugal for people who want a little more time in each place.

What we are here to do

Make fewer decisions
feel more connected.

Since 2019, our brief has stayed deliberately focused: take the time someone has, understand the days they enjoy and arrange a route that makes sense around both.

One planning contact gathers the brief and coordinates the work. The wider team supports route research, stay-area options and the practical questions behind a movement day.

We work in the middle-to-upper-mid-market range, where a comfortable local stay and a well-paced week matter more than adding a prestige label to every stop.

How the work is agreed
The principles we plan by

Stay curious.
Keep the itinerary readable.

A place needs time.

We favour fewer bases and deliberate open days. Repeating a good neighbourhood is a valid part of a trip, not a gap to fill with more distance.

A route has trade-offs.

Every sample says what it leaves out. Keeping a week focused on two cities means another region waits for another journey.

A promise needs a boundary.

Planning is separate from operating a train, running a hotel or confirming a table. We mark the choices and checks that belong with the traveller and provider.

Who we plan for

Good company,
in a manageable group.

Couples, families with two adults and one or two children, and private parties of three to six adults. We start with the people who will share the trip and the pace that works for them.

Our online samples do not cover groups of seven or more, airline operation, fixed-departure tours or an unlimited concierge arrangement.
