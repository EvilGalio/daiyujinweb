Why this route works as a starting point

A change of scene,
with time on either side.

Three nights in Bilbao make room for its river and a chosen collection. A single move leads to four nights in San Sebastián, where the bay and neighbourhood food can be enjoyed without changing stays again.

The days, in order

A week with
some breathing space.

One suggested focus at a time. Arrival, movement and departure days stay clear of timed commitments. Exact visits, stays and transport are selected in a separate planning conversation.

  1. 01
    Day 1 · Bilbao · Arrival

    Arrive beside the river

    Settle into your selected stay area. Keep only a short orientation in mind, with no timed activity tied to the arrival journey.

  2. 02
    Day 2 · Bilbao · City culture

    Architecture and one collection

    Spend a morning on riverside architecture and choose one collection if it appeals. Admission, opening and any guiding would be arranged separately.

  3. 03
    Day 3 · Bilbao · Food & time open

    Food at neighbourhood pace

    Make a few independent food stops, with a table or quiet break between them. The afternoon remains yours.

  4. 04
    Day 4 · San Sebastián · Road transfer

    From river to bay

    Move between the two bases using a separately arranged road transfer. Check provider and actual pickup details before travel; spend the rest of the day settling in.

  5. 05
    Day 5 · San Sebastián · Bay walk

    Follow the bay for a while

    Consider part of La Concha bay at a suitable walking pace. Check local conditions and keep an indoor collection or cafe pause available as an alternative.

  6. 06
    Day 6 · San Sebastián · Food & time open

    A morning led by food

    Explore neighbourhood food with the number of stops chosen around your appetite. No restaurant reservation or tasting is included in this sample.

  7. 07
    Day 7 · San Sebastián · Open day

    Keep the day open

    Go back to a part of the city you enjoyed, rest near the stay, or choose an independent local outing. There is no required additional destination.

  8. 08
    Day 8 · San Sebastián · Departure

    Onward, with time allowed

    Confirm your own onward service, pickup and luggage arrangements. No transfer time or airport connection is promised here.

San Sebastián Tourism: La Concha bay

A good conversation for…

Travellers who want food, city character and time near the sea. Families can trade a second outing for a rest break without unpicking the whole route.

What this route leaves out.

This is not a beach resort booking or a promise of swimming weather. Outdoor plans need a local conditions check and an indoor alternative. The inter-base movement is road-led, not a rail itinerary.
