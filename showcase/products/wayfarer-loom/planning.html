How we plan

Good trips need
clear connections.

Between one place and the next. Between the things you enjoy and the time you have. And between the planning work we do and the bookings you make.

01

Begin with the people

Who is travelling, how long you have and how you like a day to feel. We discuss stay preferences, comfort with walking and movement, and the decisions that matter most. There is no need to submit personal documents through this website.

02

Agree the planning work

Before bespoke work starts, the written agreement defines deliverables, planning fee, timing and how revisions are handled. Travel costs stay separate. The website does not quote a fee or confirm the studio has availability.

03

Build a route worth keeping

We arrange the bases, movement days and a shortlist of stay areas. Activity suggestions follow the interests and rhythm in the brief, with room left open and questions marked for confirmation.

04

Hand over clear decisions

The plan identifies services to check and the bookings you make directly with selected providers. The provider confirms price, availability and terms. The written agreement also sets the boundary of any later planning support.

Planning and travel are separate

Know what you are agreeing to.

The planning work

  • A route and the reasoning behind its sequence.
  • A stay-area shortlist and questions to resolve before selecting rooms.
  • Day-by-day activity suggestions, movement notes and open time.
  • A logistics checklist for your independently made bookings.

Separate travel costs

  • Flights, rail tickets, transfers and local transport.
  • Accommodation, meals, admissions and guiding.
  • Travel insurance and any required travel documents.
  • Changes or services outside the written planning agreement.
There is no fixed package price on this website. Dates, room choices, transport, admissions and provider availability change travel costs. A planning fee would be agreed before work starts; no payment is taken here.

A few useful questions.

Will you book our trains and accommodation?

We prepare the route, stay-area shortlist, activity suggestions and logistics checklist. You book directly with the providers you choose. We do not issue tickets, take a booking payment or own the accommodation.

Can a family travel at a slower pace?

Yes. The sample brief supports two adults and one or two children. A family outline makes rest breaks explicit and avoids automatically adding a second outing. Ages, room configuration, walking needs and practical arrangements would be discussed before a real plan is agreed.

Are the sample train times current?

No live train time or seat inventory is displayed. The sample routes use verified city connections as planning context. The exact train, stations, access, disruptions and tickets must be checked with the operator for your dates before purchase.

Do you provide round-the-clock support while we travel?

No unlimited concierge or 24/7 on-trip service is included. The written planning agreement defines any later support. Booked travel services and their operational support remain with the selected providers.

Can you advise on visas or travel insurance?

Those are outside this planning service. You remain responsible for the documents and cover appropriate to your situation. This website does not interpret entry rules, recommend a policy or guarantee travel eligibility.

Does the trip brief send an enquiry?

No. It creates an outline only in the current page, with no account, form submission or stored profile. You may download a text copy; refresh, navigation or clearing the form discards page choices. A download remains on your device.
