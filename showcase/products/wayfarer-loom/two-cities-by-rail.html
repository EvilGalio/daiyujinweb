Why this route works as a starting point

A change of scene,
with time on either side.

The route avoids changing stays each night. Three nights in Lisbon create a settled beginning; four in Porto leave a full open day after the intercity move.

The days, in order

A week with
some breathing space.

One suggested focus at a time. Arrival, movement and departure days stay clear of timed commitments. Exact visits, stays and transport are selected in a separate planning conversation.

  1. 01
    Day 1 · Lisbon · Arrival

    Arrive in Lisbon

    Reach the chosen neighbourhood and settle in. A nearby meal is enough of a plan for the first evening; no timed visit depends on arrival.

  2. 02
    Day 2 · Lisbon · Streets & design

    Look at the small things

    Give a morning to the street detail, tiles and buildings around one neighbourhood. Discuss a suitable walk and where to pause before fixing the route.

  3. 03
    Day 3 · Lisbon · Food & time open

    A food morning, an open afternoon

    Make a small set of independent food stops, with the afternoon left open. A restaurant booking or guided tasting is not part of the sample.

  4. 04
    Day 4 · Porto · Rail transfer

    One rail journey north

    Take a suitable CP connection once dates and seats have been verified. Allow time for both station journeys and settling into the second base.

  5. 05
    Day 5 · Porto · Riverside

    The city beside the river

    Choose a manageable riverside walk and one place to stop. Walking surfaces, slopes and access need individual consideration.

  6. 06
    Day 6 · Porto · Art & craft

    One collection and time around it

    Choose an art or craft collection to explore, with admission checked separately. Leave the remainder of the day near your base.

  7. 07
    Day 7 · Porto · Open day

    Nothing fixed in the diary

    Return to a favourite street, have a longer lunch or simply rest. This route does not need a last-minute day trip to be complete.

  8. 08
    Day 8 · Porto · Departure

    Continue from Porto

    Check your actual onward tickets and any station or airport journey separately. No connection, transfer or departure time is included.

A good conversation for…

Couples and families curious about city life, architecture and food. A good starting point when one rail journey appeals more than a car-led multi-region itinerary.

What this route leaves out.

This stays with Lisbon and Porto. It does not include the Douro Valley, Algarve or a day trip to every nearby sight. Walking distances, slopes and accommodation access must be discussed; accessibility is never assumed from a city name.
