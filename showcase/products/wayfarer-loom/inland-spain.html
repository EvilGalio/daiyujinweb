Why this route works as a starting point

A change of scene,
with time on either side.

The move is part of the journey, not something squeezed between two booked visits. Three nights in Madrid leave room for one collection and a neighbourhood morning; four in Segovia let the old town become familiar.

Shape this journey
The days, in order

A week with
some breathing space.

One suggested focus at a time. Arrival, movement and departure days stay clear of timed commitments. Exact visits, stays and transport are selected in a separate planning conversation.

  1. 01
    Day 1 · Madrid · Arrival

    Arrive in Madrid

    Keep the arrival day light. Reach your chosen stay, find a nearby place for supper and leave visits until you know how you feel.

  2. 02
    Day 2 · Madrid · Art & history

    One collection, properly

    Choose one art collection to spend time with, with entry arrangements checked separately. Leave the rest of the day for a neighbourhood walk or an earlier finish.

  3. 03
    Day 3 · Madrid · Food & time open

    A morning around the market

    Set aside a morning for independent food stops and the streets around them. The afternoon has no booked activity in this sample.

  4. 04
    Day 4 · Segovia · Rail transfer

    Change city, keep the day clear

    Take the proposed Madrid–Segovia rail connection when a suitable service is confirmed. Allow for station access and settling in; do not attach a timed visit to the transfer day.

  5. 05
    Day 5 · Segovia · Old town

    Read the city in stone

    Begin around the aqueduct and continue into the old town at a pace that suits you. Decide the extent of the walk locally; this is not a mapped walking route.

  6. 06
    Day 6 · Segovia · Food & time open

    The city at lunchtime

    A locally paced food morning, with your table choices made independently. Leave the afternoon for a return to somewhere you liked.

  7. 07
    Day 7 · Segovia · Open day

    A day without a list

    Keep this day genuinely unallocated. Stay near your base, repeat a favourite walk or rest. An extra excursion is not required to make the day worthwhile.

  8. 08
    Day 8 · Segovia · Departure

    Leave room for the onward journey

    Check your actual onward transport, transfers and check-out arrangements. This sample does not assume an airport connection or include a ticket.

A good conversation for…

Couples and families who like city culture, food and time to wander. Best for a first conversation about two settled bases rather than a list of Spain-wide highlights.

What this route leaves out.

It leaves out Barcelona, Andalusia and the coast. Walking surfaces, room access and individual needs must be discussed before any stay or visit is selected.
