Bring the useful questions.

  1. Your shop and your customers

    Think about your shop format, Canadian province and the pet-accessory categories you already carry.

  2. The right mix for your shelves

    Note the products and sizes you would like to discuss. Consider whether your customers tend to pick up a single item or a small grouping.

  3. Room to see the details

    Consider your display footprint and where a shopper will find the dimensions and care notes.

  4. Terms to confirm together

    Make a list of questions about stock, lead time, pack quantities, order terms and physical samples. These need a separate conversation before any commitment.
