Shift Handover / Example sequence: 2 weeks

One record across two shifts.

A 70-person metal parts maker with one site and two shifts. Packing questions and shared-tool handovers were travelling in separate messages.

The question
Can the receiving shift see the open work without having to reconstruct the conversation?
The work
Observe the current handover, draft a small shared record and rehearse its use with incoming and outgoing representatives. The example covers one work area only.
Still unresolved
Who will confirm the packing quantity before the next review? That is an operating decision for the team, not a consultant’s assumption.
Owner after handover
The incoming shift lead keeps the record; the packing lead owns the carton question. A supervisor reviews items that carry forward.

Planning Cadence / Example sequence: 3 weeks

A meeting with a decision to make.

A 110-person building-supplies distributor with two depots. Sales updates, stock exceptions and dispatch priorities were sharing one crowded weekly meeting.

The question
Which exceptions need a cross-depot decision, and what can be prepared before people meet?
The work
Map the current cycle, identify decisions and rehearse a short exception agenda. Leave a decision calendar and a preparation rule for the operations owner.
Still unresolved
Which depot will own the shared order exception? The meeting needs an accountable decision owner; no stock or service result is assumed.
Owner after handover
The planning coordinator prepares the exception list. Operations owns decisions that cross depot responsibilities.

A fictional 30-minute operating meeting, distinct from the 90-minute scoping workshop.

New-site Readiness / Example sequence: 4 weeks

A second site, with the open work visible.

A 60-person packaging distributor preparing its second location. The site team had task lists, but no shared view of what the receiving operations lead had accepted.

The question
What is completed, what has been accepted and what still needs a decision before handover?
The work
Connect workstream handovers, draft a readiness tracker and rehearse an open-item discussion. Technical commissioning and opening approval sit outside this example.
Still unresolved
Has the receiving lead accepted the returns process? A completed draft is not treated as operational acceptance.
Owner after handover
The site coordinator maintains the record; each receiving workstream owner confirms its own handover. The sponsor owns unresolved cross-team decisions.
