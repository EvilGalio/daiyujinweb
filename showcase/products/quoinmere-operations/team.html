Quoinmere is a 12-person independent operations consultancy. We work with regional makers and distributors that need a defined change in how everyday work is handed over.

Grounded in the
operating conversation.

Our typical planning context is a business of 30–300 colleagues with one to three sites in Ontario or neighbouring Canadian provinces. The geography, working arrangements and access still need to be agreed for each engagement.

A senior lead stays with the work from the first scoping conversation through the final handover. The team expands only for the skills and coordination that the brief needs.

We take on shift handovers, planning cadence and new-site operating readiness. Our focus is the work crossing those boundaries, rather than a promise to transform every part of the company.
