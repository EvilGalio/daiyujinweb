QUOINMERE OPERATIONS / FICTIONAL PLANNING CADENCE EXAMPLE
An operating meeting for a two-depot building-supplies distributor.
Not a diagnosis, actual customer record or promise of results.

00-10 / Confirm decisions needed today / A short decision list.
10-25 / Discuss shared exceptions / Named owners and next actions.
25-30 / Read back decisions / An agreed follow-through record.
Total: 30 minutes.

Before the meeting: the planning coordinator prepares a redacted exception list.
During the meeting: operations owns decisions crossing depot responsibilities.
After the meeting: record the owner and next action for each decision.
Open question: which depot will own the shared order exception?

This sample operating meeting is distinct from the planner's 90-minute scoping
workshop. No operational action, fee or engagement is accepted by this download.
