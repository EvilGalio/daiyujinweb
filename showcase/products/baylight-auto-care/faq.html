Package guide (TXT) ↗
Is the amount online a final quote?

No. It is an illustrative package amount in AUD, inclusive of GST, for the selected workload band. Condition, accessibility and finish are checked in person before a final scope, price and collection time are agreed.

Which vehicle size should I choose?

Small covers hatchbacks and compact sedans up to five seats. Medium covers larger sedans and five-seat crossovers. Large covers seven-seat SUVs and dual-cab utes, with the tray excluded. These are workload bands. Vans, camper conversions and heavy commercial vehicles are outside the standard range.

What if there is heavy pet hair or an embedded spill?

Choose that condition for Cabin Reset. The worksheet changes to an inspection-first note and gives no calculated total or duration. We do not automatically charge a surcharge, promise stain removal or include mould and biohazard cleanup.

Can you work on matte paint or a vinyl wrap?

The exterior packages are described for compatible gloss paint. Select the finish-review condition to prepare an inspection-only note. This website does not establish compatibility, and neither matte treatment nor vinyl-wrap treatment is included in a standard package.

What does the empty-boot option add?

Cabin Reset already includes a routine vacuum of an accessible, empty boot. The optional deep vacuum adds a focused pass, A$25 and 30 minutes. It is available only with Cabin Reset and does not remove inspection limits for heavy contamination.

Can I leave child seats and personal belongings inside?

Remove loose belongings from the areas you want cleaned. We do not remove or reinstall child seats. Areas blocked by a seat or personal items remain outside the accessible clean; ask about the scope before work begins.

Are the hours an appointment slot?

No. They are illustrative time allowances for preparing a visit. The workshop would agree handover and collection in person. Longer Surface Refresh visits may need a morning handover. There is no live calendar, courtesy car or after-hours key drop here.

Do you guarantee scratch removal or coating life?

No. Surface Refresh includes inspection, a test area and one-stage enhancement on compatible gloss paint. Deep-scratch repair, respraying, all-defect removal, multi-stage correction and a coating lifespan guarantee are outside the package.

What happens to the visit note?

The worksheet uses only your current page state. Refreshing or leaving clears the selections; no account, plate, personal contact data, payment or email is collected. Download creates a text file on your device. It does not contact the fictional workshop or reserve a visit.
