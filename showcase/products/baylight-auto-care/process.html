Our process / Clear from the start

A good detail starts
with a conversation.

The package is a starting point. We look at the actual vehicle, agree the accessible areas and finish, then set out the work before it begins.

  1. Prepare your note

    Choose a package, vehicle band and condition in our visit worksheet. It gives you a useful scope to discuss, with indicative hours and a package amount where the vehicle fits the standard range.

  2. Look at the vehicle

    At reception, walk around with a team member. Interior access, belongings, existing marks and the type of exterior finish determine what can be included. An unusual condition needs a closer review.

  3. Agree before work

    Confirm the work, price and collection plan together. If the package does not cover what you need, we explain the gap. Extra work is a separate decision; an online selection does not authorise a change.

  4. Work to the agreed scope

    For Surface Refresh, an inspection and small test area come before machine work. We stop to discuss anything that changes the agreed approach, rather than assume every finish can be treated in the same way.

  5. Review at collection

    Take a final walk-around, see which areas were completed and discuss any remaining marks or excluded work. The collection time is agreed with the workshop, not allocated by this website.

Before you arrive

A little preparation
makes room to work.

Empty personal belongings from the areas you want cleaned. We do not remove or reinstall child seats; anything they block remains outside the accessible clean.

Choose a weekday handover preference and allow time for an inspection. We do not offer after-hours key drop, a courtesy vehicle or mobile detailing.

Workshop hours & practicalities ↗

Package preparation

  • Cabin Reset. Remove loose belongings and empty the boot. We do not remove or reinstall child seats; any areas they block stay outside the clean.
  • Wash & Protect. Remove detachable personal items from the exterior if appropriate. Tell the workshop about any known finish changes or damage before work.
  • Surface Refresh. Allow time for a walk-around and paint discussion. A photograph or description alone cannot establish what the finish will tolerate.
