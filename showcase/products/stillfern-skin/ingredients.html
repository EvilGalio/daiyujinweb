Read before you choose

Nothing in small print.

Start with the product format, then the complete list. Ingredient names are information to inspect, not a promise about your skin.

Three different jobs.

Gentle Cleanser is a gel that is rinsed off. Daily Moisture is a face cream that stays on. Hand Balm is a cream intended for the hands. Each product page keeps its use direction beside its pack and price.

These descriptions help you compare what the products are for. They do not diagnose a skin concern or recommend a treatment.
