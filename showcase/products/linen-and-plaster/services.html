Before you begin.

Do you build or manage the contractor?

No. We develop the agreed interior design package. Construction, site supervision, contractor management, fabrication and installation are separately appointed. The appointed team checks dimensions, services and technical requirements.

Can you work with furniture I already own?

Yes. A retained-object list is part of the furnishing edit. Dimensions, photographs and a note about how each object is used help us understand what deserves a place in the room. You do not upload them to this demo.

Can a materials consultation become a larger project?

It can become the starting point for a separate room design package, if the scope is suitable and the studio has capacity. That work, its fee and timing must be agreed in writing; the consultation does not reserve a later project slot.

Do you work outside Melbourne?

Our current service area is metropolitan Melbourne. The project planner flags other locations so there is no assumption of remote or travel coverage.

What happens to my project planner choices?

They remain only in the current page. Nothing is submitted, booked or emailed. Refreshing, leaving or clearing the page discards the result; a downloaded text brief stays on your device until you delete it.
