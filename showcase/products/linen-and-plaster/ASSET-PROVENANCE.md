# Asset provenance — Linen & Plaster

All room imagery was generated for this fictional project using the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-09-11, with one call per image and no source images. Original PNGs remain at their generated location and are copied in assets/images. WebP derivatives at 768 and 1536px are lossily encoded from those originals. The images illustrate concept interiors, not completed work. All three original files were inspected with view_image before use. No external reference-site image, logo, copy or source code is included.

## kitchen

Original: assets/images/kitchen-original.png; display: kitchen-768.webp and kitchen-1536.webp.

Prompt (verbatim):

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hero and full project photograph for Linen & Plaster, a fictional six-person Melbourne interior design studio. Create an original tactile editorial interior photograph, horizontal 3:2, of a believable 26 square metre kitchen and dining area in a modest Fitzroy North terrace extension. The key existing object is a beautifully worn round oak dining table with three simple timber chairs, near a tall original timber-framed window. Existing warm terracotta square floor tiles remain visible. A new practical kitchen runs along the back and right wall: pale natural oak flat-front lower cabinetry, a thin brushed stainless steel worktop, soft butter-coloured square tile backsplash, modest unbranded fixtures. Plaster walls are a warm chalky off-white. Everyday ceramics on an open shelf, one linen tea towel, small bowl of pears, a clear working surface. No enormous island. A lived-in family room for two adults and a child, not a luxury showroom or sterile minimalist gallery. Soft late-morning natural daylight, warm cream, oak, terracotta and subdued olive accents. Eye-level architectural photography from the dining-room side; coherent room proportions and cabinet perspective. Wide overall view with kitchen and retained table clearly legible, useful subject areas through centre and right, room for a small website headline in lower-left over image if needed. No people, no logos, no readable text, no watermark, no copied design, no impossible fixtures, no additional furniture blocking the circulation.

## reading

Original: assets/images/reading-original.png; display: reading-768.webp and reading-1536.webp.

Prompt (verbatim):

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full project photograph for Linen & Plaster, a fictional Melbourne interior design studio. Create an original warm editorial photograph, horizontal 3:2, of a 17 square metre reading and occasional-home-working room in Brunswick East. The protagonist is an existing brick-red upholstered armchair with natural signs of use, angled gently toward a tall sash window with natural linen curtains. A small oak side table holds a book and a cup beside the chair. A low muted-olive cabinet with a handful of well-used books, a simple unbranded floor lamp, a textured neutral woven rug and off-white plaster walls complete a comfortable, inhabited room. Keep a visible passage through the room and do not overcrowd with new furniture. The room should feel personal and useful rather than staged as a glossy showroom. Soft afternoon side light, warm milk, rust red, olive, oak and linen palette; subtle tactile surface detail and realistic object scale. Eye-level interior photography with clear view of chair, lamp, side table, rug and window. No people, no logos, no readable writing, no watermark, no luxury designer-product imitation, no architectural intervention or dramatic futuristic furniture.

## bakery

Original: assets/images/bakery-original.png; display: bakery-768.webp and bakery-1536.webp.

Prompt (verbatim):

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full project photograph for Linen & Plaster, a fictional Melbourne interior design studio. Create an original editorial interior photograph, horizontal 3:2, of the 38 square metre front-of-house area of a small neighbourhood bakery in Carlton. A restrained pale oak service counter has a compact glazed bread/pastry display at one end and a separate clear collection landing at the other, set against warm cream tiled walls and a rust-red linen curtain to the back area. Retain two slightly worn bentwood stools beside a narrow wall ledge near the original tall shopfront glazing. Small neat bread baskets, a few unbranded white ceramic plates and a simple warm pendant; an ordinary useful shop, not a luxury restaurant. Clear central floor area for a short customer queue; pale warm tile floor, tactile oak front, soft plaster above tiled wall. Natural early morning light enters from the shopfront. Camera from inside the entrance looking toward counter, plausible compact proportions and human scale. Inviting mid-market local character, no people, no signage or readable labels, no real brand marks, no watermark, no compliance or certification symbols, no excessive floral styling or neon.
