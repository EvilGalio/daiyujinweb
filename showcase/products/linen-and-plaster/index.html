Before we choose what is new

We ask what
should stay.

A table with room for everyone. The chair that is already comfortable. A window that sets the mood of the room. These are useful starting points, not obstacles to a new design.

Then we make the next decisions together: how the room works, what it needs, and what can be left alone.
