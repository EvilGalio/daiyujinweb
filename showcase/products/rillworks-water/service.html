FROM FIRST REVIEW TO ROUTINE CARE

A system needs
a workable handover.

We plan the operator’s part alongside the equipment. Access, consumables, records and responsibilities are decisions to make before installation.

01

Source review

Describe the source, intended use, operating demand and available evidence. We identify missing information and agree what is needed before proposing a stage. Sampling and laboratory work remain separately arranged.

02

Scope & layout

Agree the duty and interfaces, then develop the equipment arrangement, pipe routes and maintenance access. The quote separates equipment, integration, commissioning and any planned service.

03

Integration & handover

Carry out the agreed installation scope and record the selected settings and consumables. Walk through the equipment instructions, operator checks and agreed escalation points with the site team.

04

Planned care

Arrange service against the selected equipment and site conditions. Maintain a record of work and review material changes to the source or operating duty; the original brief is not a permanent assumption.

BEFORE WE VISIT

A few practical answers.

Can you choose a filter from a photograph?

A photograph helps with access and pipework context. It does not establish water composition or treatment suitability. We also need the source, intended duty and available evidence.

Do you maintain equipment supplied by someone else?

We can discuss a limited review when the equipment identity, instructions and service history are available. A visit or ongoing service is only agreed after checking the scope; universal brand support is not promised.

How often will maintenance be needed?

The schedule depends on the selected equipment, source conditions and operating duty. We agree project-specific checks and triggers at handover rather than publish one interval for every system.

Does this website arrange a site visit?

No. The source planner prepares a local demonstration checklist. It does not send an enquiry, reserve an appointment or dispatch a technician.
