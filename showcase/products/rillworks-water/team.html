HOW THE TEAM FITS TOGETHER

Different roles.
A shared project record.

Site review

Brings the source history, intended use and operating constraints together. Identifies the questions that need evidence before a proposal.

Mechanical integration

Works through pipework interfaces, equipment placement and service access within the agreed project scope.

Commissioning & handover

Records the agreed setup and helps the operator understand selected equipment instructions, checks and consumables.

Service coordination

Keeps the service scope and work record connected to the original duty, so changes can be reviewed in context.
