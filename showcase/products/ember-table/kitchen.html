The idea behind the table

Small enough
to know the menu.

Ember Table began in 2022 with a simple idea: build dinner around the hearth and keep the room small enough for a conversation. Our fictional Portland team has twelve people: seven in the kitchen and five looking after the dining room.

We put vegetables at the centre of the menu because texture and heat give us plenty to work with. We also cook chicken and trout. This is a restaurant for a mixed table of appetites, not a strictly vegetarian kitchen.

What matters on the plate.

01

Char, then balance

Fire brings dark edges and depth. Lemon tahini, cider dressing and bitter leaves keep richer plates from feeling heavy.

02

A short, complete menu

Six dishes means every option has a place. The three-course vegetarian set gives you a ready-made path; individual plates let you make your own.

03

Drinks for dinner

Apple and rosemary, lemon verbena, tart cherry. Our three alcohol-free recipes are made to sit alongside food, with smaller pours for the set pairing.

End on a gentle note

Leave room
for the pear.

Soft fruit, a spoonful of cream, a little crunch. We like a dessert that feels familiar, with enough contrast to make the last bite a good one.
