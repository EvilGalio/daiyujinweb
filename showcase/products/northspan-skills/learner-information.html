For learners / Before you arrive

A clear start feels different.

Know what to bring, what is already here and what to ask about. You do not need to arrive with everything figured out.

Check the starting point.

All classes are for adults aged 18 and over and are taught in English. Basic arithmetic and following written instructions matter across the three courses.

CAD needs everyday computer confidence. Planning also needs basic spreadsheet entry and sorting. Drawing Reading is open to learners without CAD or workshop experience.

Compare your starting point

Bring curiosity. We have the kit.

Computers and classroom software access are provided for CAD and Planning. Worksheets, a notebook and stationery are included for every learner.

Bring a water bottle and lunch if you wish. No personal laptop, software purchase or workshop clothing is required. These courses do not involve powered-machine operation.

Make time for the whole course.

Day classes run 09:30–16:30: six taught hours with one hour of breaks. The CAD evening format runs 18:00–21:00 on four Tuesdays. Each session builds on the previous one.

If you cannot attend a full published cohort, use “Discuss another date” in the planner. It starts a timing discussion; it does not promise a replacement session.

It is fine to ask how learning will work.

Ask about practical arrangements before committing time or money.

Reading and pace

Ask about print size, written instructions and how much independent reading each exercise involves. We can discuss the format; this site does not collect support or health details.

Using the room

Ask about the route into the classroom, seating and breaks. The centre description is illustrative; do not use this concept site to plan a real visit.

What you take away

Your practice files or worksheets and tutor feedback on the exercise. There is no degree, accredited award, job placement or promise of workplace competence.

About booking terms: this demonstration does not take bookings or payments. In a real enquiry, fees, availability, any changes and cancellation terms would need to be supplied and agreed before enrolment. Nothing here creates an enrolment.
