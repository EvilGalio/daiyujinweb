Our centre / Birmingham, UK

A small centre.
Room to work things out.

Northspan began in 2019 with a straightforward idea: short technical courses should give adults time to try, ask and revise.

Learning around the table

Useful work, close attention.

Our two classrooms and supervised teaching bench room are arranged around the exercise. In one session that means a drawing on a screen. In another it is a paper job list and a pencil.

Eight tutors, four learner coordinators and three operations colleagues keep the teaching and practical arrangements connected. We focus on three introductory courses, with a clear finish to each one.

The centre serves adults and local employer teams in Birmingham. All published teaching is in English and in the classroom.

Who helps with what

Clear responsibilities matter more than a long list of titles.

Teaching / 8 people

The practice team

Tutors demonstrate the exercise, make time for questions and give feedback on the work produced in class. No professional qualifications are claimed in this concept.

Learner coordination / 4 people

The first conversation

Coordinators help clarify the course, starting point and schedule, and identify practical arrangements to discuss before joining.

Operations / 3 people

The room behind the lesson

Operations colleagues prepare classroom computers, paper materials and the daily room setup so that class time is used for learning.

Arranging a visit

In a real enquiry, ask for the confirmed location, arrival instructions and access arrangements before travelling. This fictional centre has no public street address and does not accept real visits.

Office rhythm: Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00. The published CAD evening cohort has its own teaching times.

A useful boundary

We teach introductory knowledge and practice. We do not issue degrees, claim government accreditation, operate a machine qualification scheme or promise employment.
