Two ways to frame the conversation

One course.
The right group shape.

The three published courses have defined exercises, starting requirements and duration. We keep those boundaries visible for employers too.

There is no unlimited custom curriculum hidden behind a group enquiry.

Join a published cohort

For learners with the same starting point who can attend the full schedule. CAD and Planning have up to eight learners; Drawing Reading has up to ten. Published dates do not show remaining places.

Discuss a separate group

For a team that needs another date, or has more people than the normal class size. The planner accepts 1–12 learners and flags where a split or separate arrangement must be discussed.

Use the same reference fees

The note multiplies the published per-learner fee by your group count. It does not apply automatic discounts or quote a private course. Scheduling and any separate-group terms remain open.

A better brief for your team

  1. Name the task

    Drawing a simple part, reading a drawing or explaining a short production plan. Select one course for each preparation note.

  2. Check a shared starting point

    Use the least experienced learner as the starting point. If the group varies, discuss separate learning needs rather than assuming one pace fits everyone.

  3. Separate preference from confirmation

    Bring a preferred cohort, learner count and practical questions. Availability, timing and scope still need agreement before any real enrolment.
