NS 01 / Draw it clearly.

Intro to CAD

Start with lines and circles. Leave with a dimensioned 2D drawing you can explain.

£240 per learner12 taught hoursUp to 8 learners

Your starting point

Use a mouse, open files and organise folders confidently. No previous CAD experience is needed.

What you will practise

  • Construct and edit basic 2D geometry.
  • Use layers and dimensions to keep a drawing readable.
  • Check your drawing against a short brief and export it for review.

How the time is used

Session 1 · 3 hours
Files, coordinates, lines and circles. Build the outline of the plate.
Session 2 · 3 hours
Position the holes and make deliberate edits. Practise undoing and revising.
Session 3 · 3 hours
Add layers, dimensions and a clear drawing layout.
Session 4 · 3 hours
Check against the brief, exchange feedback and export the revision.
An exercise you can see

Draw it clearly.

Draw a 100 × 60 mm plate, place two 10 mm holes and add the dimensions another reader needs. Revise the hole position, then export a drawing for review.

Download the plate exercise (SVG)

An introductory 2D exercise, not a manufacturing-ready design or a software qualification.

NS 02 / Read before you make.

Workshop Drawing Reading

Find the dimensions that matter, and the missing information worth asking about.

£135 per learner6 taught hoursUp to 10 learners

Your starting point

Be comfortable with basic arithmetic and following written English. No CAD or workshop experience is required.

What you will practise

  • Identify overall dimensions, views and centre positions.
  • Separate a stated dimension from an assumption.
  • Write a concise clarification question before work begins.

How the time is used

Morning · 3 hours
Units, views, lines and overall dimensions. Work through the plate drawing in pairs.
Afternoon · 3 hours
Locate the holes, identify missing information and practise a handover conversation.
An exercise you can see

Read before you make.

Read a plate drawing, locate hole centres and separate given dimensions from assumptions. Write three questions that would need answering before someone made the part.

Download the reading worksheet (TXT)

Reading practice only. No machine operation, safety qualification or permission to manufacture a part.

NS 03 / See what fits the day.

Production Planning Essentials

Turn a short job list into a capacity conversation, with assumptions out in the open.

£285 per learner12 taught hoursUp to 8 learners

Your starting point

Use a computer and enter and sort rows in a spreadsheet. You do not need advanced formulas or planning software.

What you will practise

  • Separate setup time, unit time and available capacity.
  • Build and explain a simple load calculation.
  • Compare choices and record the assumptions behind a plan.

How the time is used

Session 1 · 3 hours
Define the job list, units, setup time and the planning window.
Session 2 · 3 hours
Calculate load in a spreadsheet and check the total with a partner.
Session 3 · 3 hours
Compare the load with available time and surface the capacity gap.
Session 4 · 3 hours
Discuss sequencing options and prepare a short assumptions-led handover.
An exercise you can see

See what fits the day.

Compare three jobs totalling 410 minutes with a 360-minute slot. Find the 50-minute gap, then explain what must change instead of hiding the difference.

Download the planning data (CSV)

A simplified classroom model, not an operational factory schedule or an optimisation guarantee.
