KC-14

Four screws. One enclosure.

A locating fixture and screw-fastening station for a single enclosure variant. The brief begins with screw access and a repeatable seated position.

Parts
One enclosure variant
Concept envelope
1.8 × 1.4 m
Operator role
Load, remove and inspect
Defined output
Locating nest + fastening sequence

The constraint

The enclosure must seat without distorting its walls. The fixture needs access to all four screws while keeping loading straightforward.

What we would trial

Trial representative enclosures and fasteners. Establish the acceptable joint criteria with the product team, then record the agreed cycle target under those conditions.

What handover must cover

Show the operator how to recognise an incorrectly seated part and route it for review. Include fixture drawings, wear-item references and the agreed recovery sequence.

The envelope is illustrative, not an installation clearance or safety specification. Cycle time is established during trial, not promised here.

KC-22

The tray is part of the system.

A tray-fed loader serving one existing CNC machine, with two tray patterns. The project keeps replenishment and first-off approval in the operator workflow.

Parts
Two tray patterns
Concept envelope
2.4 × 1.8 m
Operator role
Replenish + approve first-off
Defined output
Part presentation + machine interface

The constraint

Gripper access, part orientation and the machine interface need to work together. Swarf and the condition of returning parts belong in the trial, not just a clean sample demonstration.

What we would trial

Review machine permissions with the owner. Run a representative tray change and agree the response to a missing, misoriented or rejected part before specifying the build.

What handover must cover

Train the maintenance team on the documented interfaces and accessible wear items. Agree who owns tray setup, tool changes and machine-specific faults.

The envelope is illustrative, not an installation clearance or safety specification. Cycle time is established during trial, not promised here.

KC-31

A clear seat. A clear result.

A benchtop connector-test fixture for two keyed variants. The product owner supplies the test recipe; the fixture guides connection and separates exceptions from completed tests.

Parts
Two keyed variants
Concept envelope
0.8 × 0.7 m
Operator role
Handle cables + review rejects
Defined output
Locating fixture + test interface

The constraint

Similar connectors can have different keying. Variant selection and contact access must be explicit, including what happens when a part does not seat.

What we would trial

Use approved samples and the product team’s test recipe. Demonstrate variant identification, incomplete seating and an agreed set of known result conditions.

What handover must cover

Document contact replacement and fixture inspection. Show operators how to route an incomplete test, and make clear who reviews a reject.

The envelope is illustrative, not an installation clearance or safety specification. Cycle time is established during trial, not promised here.
