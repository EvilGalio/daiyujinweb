Kestrel Cellworks brings mechanical engineering, controls and project delivery together around defined manufacturing tasks. We are a 24-person team serving the Benelux.

Our way of working

The right people
stay in the room.

A mechanical question can become an operator question. A controls decision can change the way maintenance works.

Our project leads keep those conversations together. We work with your engineering and operations teams, define the interfaces we own and identify those retained by the machine supplier or site team.

Since 2018, our focus has been bounded assembly, handling and test tasks. We do not need to claim a whole factory to make one operation clearer.
