Working together

A decision at
every stage.

A build is a commitment. We use feasibility to resolve the largest unknowns first, then carry the agreed scope through engineering, acceptance and handover.

01

Define the operation

You bring the part, the current process and the reason to change it. We identify the retained manual work, machine interfaces and unresolved assumptions.

Output: a bounded task and the questions for feasibility.
02

Test feasibility

A paid, agreed scope lets us inspect samples, examine access and test the uncertain steps. We agree the evidence needed before either side commits to a build.

Output: findings, constraints and a decision to proceed, revise or stop.
03

Engineer and build

Mechanical, controls and project leads work from the agreed scope. Changes to variants, interfaces or acceptance conditions are reviewed before they become build changes.

Output: the cell, fixtures and documentation defined in the contract.
04

Agree acceptance

We test the agreed parts and sequences against the conditions established during engineering. Operator tasks, exceptions and site responsibilities remain part of the review.

Output: an acceptance record and any specifically assigned open actions.
05

Hand over the working knowledge

Operators practise normal use and the agreed recovery steps. Maintenance receives drawings, wear-item references and responsibility boundaries. Support is scoped for the installed equipment.

Output: training, documentation and an agreed aftercare route.
Before the first conversation

Bring the real
conditions.

Representative parts are more useful than a perfect rendering. Include known variant differences, typical defects, current manual steps and the restrictions of the space where the task happens.

  • Part drawings and samples you are authorised to share.
  • A current sequence, including inspection and rework.
  • Machine interface ownership and available site information.
  • The people who will operate, accept and maintain the cell.

Site-specific engineering and safety work cannot be settled by this website. The online brief is a preparation aid only.
