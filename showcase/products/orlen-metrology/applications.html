Three representative workshop questions. Each begins with a feature, a setup and a limit to investigate.

Profile edge / Turned valve pin

Can the shoulder and external edge be seen in the same setup?

A small turned valve pin has several external shoulders. The quality team wants to understand whether the drawing's edge can be seen clearly and held consistently.

Proposed starting point
O300 / Optical comparator.
What the demonstration should establish
Hold a representative part so the feature is visible in silhouette. The demonstration considers orientation, lighting, magnification and how the operator identifies the edge.
What may change the recommendation
Stage travel is not a guaranteed sample envelope. Fixture size and lens field of view change what can be inspected. Internal features, full 3D geometry and automatic report export sit outside this configuration.

Step height / Milled spacer block

Is the upper face accessible from a repeatable reference?

A milled spacer block has an accessible upper face. The team wants to compare the step height from a chosen reference surface without creating an unnecessarily complex inspection routine.

Proposed starting point
G20 / Digital height gauge.
What the demonstration should establish
Agree the reference face, support the workpiece and choose an accessible contact point. The demonstration looks at the complete setup, including the surface plate and workholding.
What may change the recommendation
The display increment describes the screen step; it is not a calibrated accuracy or uncertainty statement. Soft samples, inaccessible surfaces and dimensions beyond the travel need a different review.

Push / pull / Snap-fit cap

What force is needed in the intended assembly direction?

A plastic cap clicks onto a rigid body. The assembly team wants to compare the force required to seat it, in the same direction used by the operator.

Proposed starting point
F8 / Manual force stand kit.
What the demonstration should establish
Define the loading direction and anticipated force, then select grips or contact fixtures. The demonstration considers alignment, sample support and the operator's test method.
What may change the recommendation
The stand alone does not measure force; the matched gauge does. This kit does not offer automated fatigue cycling, a force/displacement trace or materials certification. The usable sample space depends on installed fixtures.
