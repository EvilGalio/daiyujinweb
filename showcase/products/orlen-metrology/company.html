Our Brno team brings instrument assembly, application work and distributor support together. The range stays deliberately focused: external optical profiles, accessible contact heights and straightforward manual force comparisons.

We work with workshops and manufacturing quality teams across Czechia, Slovakia, Poland and Austria. Demonstration arrangements and aftercare are coordinated through distributors in those markets.
