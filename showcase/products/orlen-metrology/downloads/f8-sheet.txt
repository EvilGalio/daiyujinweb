A handwheel-operated stand paired with a matched force gauge for straightforward push and pull comparisons. Fixtures define how force is applied to your sample.

Matched force gauge: 0–500 N range
Crosshead travel: 200 mm
Base footprint: 250 × 300 mm
Actuation: Manual handwheel
Fixtures: Selected for the sample
Results: Peak force on gauge; manual record

Setup: Define the loading direction and anticipated force, then select grips or contact fixtures. The demonstration considers alignment, sample support and the operator's test method.
Included: Manual stand with a matched 500 N gauge. A fixture proposal is prepared with the sample; specialised grips are scoped separately.
Limits: The stand alone does not measure force; the matched gauge does. This kit does not offer automated fatigue cycling, a force/displacement trace or materials certification. The usable sample space depends on installed fixtures.
Support: Fixture adapters and replacement contact tools are matched to the agreed kit. Sensor checks and calibration are separate from mechanical stand support.

All specifications are fictional selection data. No calibrated accuracy, uncertainty, certification or compliance claim is made. Samples and display readings in images are illustrative.
