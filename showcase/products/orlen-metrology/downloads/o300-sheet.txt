ORLEN METROLOGY / O300
Fictional company and concept instrument sheet

A horizontal optical comparator for viewing the external silhouette of a small part on a projection screen. Use the manual XY stage and readout to explore a defined feature.

Projection screen: 300 mm diameter
XY stage travel: 150 × 100 mm
Lens options: 10× / 20×
Stage load: Up to 5 kg
Bench footprint: 760 × 520 mm
Results: Digital XY readout; manual record

Setup: Hold a representative part so the feature is visible in silhouette. The demonstration considers orientation, lighting, magnification and how the operator identifies the edge.
Included: Comparator, selected lens and XY readout. Workholding and any additional lens are scoped with the application.
Limits: Stage travel is not a guaranteed sample envelope. Fixture size and lens field of view change what can be inspected. Internal features, full 3D geometry and automatic report export sit outside this configuration.
Support: Discuss illumination modules, lens care accessories and fixture interfaces with the supplying distributor.

All specifications are fictional selection data. No calibrated accuracy, uncertainty, certification or compliance claim is made. Samples and display readings in images are illustrative.
