ORLEN METROLOGY / G20
Fictional company and concept instrument sheet

A compact contact height gauge for accessible faces on a stable workpiece. Set it on a suitable surface plate and bring the probe to the feature you need to compare.

Vertical travel: 0–300 mm
Display increment: 0.01 mm
Base footprint: 210 × 160 mm
Method: Contact probe
Reference surface: Suitable surface plate required
Results: Operator-read; manual record

Setup: Agree the reference face, support the workpiece and choose an accessible contact point. The demonstration looks at the complete setup, including the surface plate and workholding.
Included: G20 body and standard contact probe. Surface plate, reference artifacts and application-specific tips are selected separately.
Limits: The display increment describes the screen step; it is not a calibrated accuracy or uncertainty statement. Soft samples, inaccessible surfaces and dimensions beyond the travel need a different review.
Support: Probe tips and compatible contact accessories are identified by the instrument reference. Calibration is arranged through the customer's chosen qualified provider.

All specifications are fictional selection data. No calibrated accuracy, uncertainty, certification or compliance claim is made. Samples and display readings in images are illustrative.
