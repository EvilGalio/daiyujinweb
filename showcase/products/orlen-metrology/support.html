Selection & ownership

A few useful distinctions.

Does a finer display mean greater accuracy?

No. A display increment tells you the step shown on a screen. It does not establish accuracy or measurement uncertainty for your application. The setup, method and appropriate verification still need to be considered.

Does the F8 stand measure force by itself?

No. The stand controls the movement and supports the fixture. The matched force gauge measures the applied force. Gauge range, alignment and fixture selection are part of the application review.

Can these instruments create automatic reports?

The configurations shown here use operator-recorded results. The O300 has an XY readout; G20 readings and the F8 gauge peak are read by the operator. Automated export and force/displacement traces are outside this range.

How are accessories and wear items identified?

Use the model and accessory reference with the supplying distributor. We can scope probe tips, fixture adapters and illumination components appropriate to the instrument. Compatibility and availability are confirmed per request.

Who arranges calibration?

The customer chooses a suitable qualified provider and a verification plan for the application. Orlen is not presented as an accredited calibration laboratory, and this website issues no certificates.
