Before choosing

A few practical answers.

What if our team needs more than 15 users?

Start with a scope conversation. The two published plans cover up to 15 users in one workspace. We would need to understand a larger team's order volume, responsibilities and workspace needs before proposing a fit.

Does this replace our warehouse or accounting system?

No. Tallyharbor focuses on purchase-order follow-up, supplier documents and arrival changes. Warehouse stock, accounting, freight booking and customs work remain in the systems and services that own them.

What should we bring to the walkthrough?

Prepare representative field names, the kinds of documents you handle and the steps in your current handover. Use anonymised examples. The website's agenda builder does not request personal details, files or live order data.

Are live carrier integrations included in the demo?

No. All arrivals in the demo are synthetic and manually editable. It has no carrier, accounting or supplier connection. Any proposed integration would need its own feasibility and scope agreement.
