ND-14 / COMPONENT RECORD

Manifold body

A compact aluminium interface that routes air within a bench automation assembly. The drawing starts with a mounting face and defines the port positions from the same datum.

Example specification
MaterialAluminium 6082-T6
Envelope120 × 80 × 44 mm
Example batch100 pieces
RouteMilling, drilling and specified threading
FinishDeburred, as machined; visible faces agreed at review

What we would inspect

The mounting face is the primary datum. Port positions, thread depth and the seal interface are the named inspection priorities; no pressure rating is implied by this concept part.

Which ports are critical to assembly, and does the drawing specify a test method beyond dimensional inspection? Leak or pressure testing is not included in this example.

ND-28 / COMPONENT RECORD

Fixture plate

A repeatable base for a small assembly fixture. Twelve M6 fixing points locate adjustable tooling without requiring a new plate for each product variant.

Example specification
MaterialAluminium 6082-T6
Envelope180 × 120 × 16 mm
Example batch50 pieces
RouteFace milling, drilling and tapping
FinishDeburred, as machined; anodising quoted separately if requested

What we would inspect

The hole pattern and locating face are reviewed together. Thread depth is checked against the drawing, and burr removal is agreed for the assembly-facing edges.

Does the fixture locate from its outside edges or from a separate datum feature? That decision changes what must be controlled and inspected.

CONCEPT DRAWING / NOT TO SCALE

The radial feature is a secondary operation. Its relationship to the locating face is included in the review so it does not become an assumption on the shop floor.

ND-32 / COMPONENT RECORD

Sensor collar

A turned collar that locates a non-safety-critical sensor in an equipment housing. A radial fixing secures the sensor after its axial position is set.

Example specification
MaterialStainless 303
Envelope48 mm outside diameter × 30 mm long; 26 mm nominal bore
Example batch200 pieces
RouteTurning and boring, followed by a radial tapped hole
FinishDeburred, as machined; bore finish specified on the drawing

What we would inspect

The bore-to-face relationship takes priority. The final bore fit and surface requirement must be specified for the actual sensor; the nominal dimensions here are not a fit recommendation.

What locates the sensor axially, and what bore fit does the assembly require? A sample measurement does not replace a controlled drawing.
