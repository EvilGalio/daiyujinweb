01 / CNC MILLING

Flat faces.
Purposeful features.

We mill bodies, plates and equipment interfaces from billet. The first review looks at how to locate the component, reach its features and measure what matters after machining.

Deep pockets, thin walls and features across multiple setups need individual review. We do not infer a tolerance from a CAD model alone.

A CLEAR BOUNDARY

Useful to know
before you enquire.

We are set up for repeat, small-batch components for equipment builders. We do not offer casting, sheet-metal fabrication, regulated aerospace work or medical implants.

Machining and dimensional inspection stay in-house. Anodising and other agreed finishes are coordinated with external partners; their process and lead time form part of a specific quote.

Bring these four things

  1. The current drawing revision and a matching model.
  2. Material, finish and quantity per delivery.
  3. Critical features and the inspection record you need.
  4. Your requested date and any repeat-order pattern.
