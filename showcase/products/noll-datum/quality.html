THE INSPECTION PLAN

Make the critical
features explicit.

A drawing can contain hundreds of dimensions. We agree the critical features, the required record and the sampling approach for the job, instead of implying that every part receives every possible measurement.

There is no universal tolerance promise. Dimensions, fits and surface requirements must be specified and accepted for the actual component.

Include inspection in your enquiry ↗

  1. Control the starting point

    Review the model and drawing together. Resolve revision differences, missing datums and open notes before releasing work.

  2. Check the first setup

    Confirm the nominated features against the accepted drawing. Agree whether a first-off record or customer review is needed before continuing.

  3. Check the batch

    Use the agreed inspection method and sampling plan during machining. A requirement for full inspection must be stated and quoted.

  4. Keep the handover legible

    Identify the batch and revision, include the agreed dimensional record, and flag any approved deviations separately.

PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before the first batch.

Can we change the drawing after placing an order?

Ask your project engineer to review the change before work continues. The revised scope, any already completed operations and the resulting cost or timing changes need agreement. We do not quietly substitute a new revision into an active job.

Do all parts receive a dimensional report?

No. The quotation states the required record and inspection scope. A first-off record, sampled feature report and a report for every part are different requirements and must be priced and planned accordingly.

Do you hold an industry certification?

No industry certification or accreditation is claimed on this website. If your purchasing process requires a specific certification, raise it before requesting a quote; do not infer approval from a sample handover document.

Can you carry out performance or pressure testing?

Our declared scope is machining and dimensional inspection. Functional, pressure, leak, electrical and regulated validation tests are not included. Any need for them must be handled through a separately agreed competent provider.
